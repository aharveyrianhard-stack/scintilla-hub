THE CURVE · BEFORE AND AFTER

Both sides hold the same thirteen names with the same numbers and re-sort at the same instant. The only difference between them is the easing curve. Left is what the hub did until now. Right is what the motion spec says. Press the button a few times, then slow it down.

HUB BEFORE cubic-bezier(.32, 0, .18, 1) · 420ms
SPEC — NOW IN THE HUB cubic-bezier(.34, .02, .2, 1) · 400ms

What to look for: the first instant of the move, not the landing. The left side's curve starts at exactly 0, so a row leaves from a dead stop and gets snatched into motion. The right side starts at .02 — a fraction of ease-in — so the row already carries a little weight as it goes. Both arrive at about the same time. It is the departure that differs.
