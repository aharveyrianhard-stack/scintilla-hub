Source audit · not equivalence certified

Pivots before patterns

Trendoscope starts from rolling highs/lows, not the symmetric confirmed pivots used by the channel scripts. Our Pivot Atom already includes rolling-extreme candidates, but its custom zigzag confirmation rules are different.

The distinction that matters

Candidate coverage is not selected-sequence parity. The local pivot union has symmetric windows, trailing extremes, flips, run completion, smoothed extremes and custom zigzag lanes. Exact anchors, equal-price handling, confirmation times and recursive selection need comparison before calling it a Trendoscope match.
