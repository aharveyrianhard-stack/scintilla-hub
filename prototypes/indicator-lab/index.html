Names, not mystery initials

What the combined preview shows

Indigo: RSI · Pink: Stochastic %K · Teal: Williams %R +100.

The preview starts with RSI and %K on, Williams off. “8H K” means Stochastic %K calculated on eight-hour candles. Overlapping translucent colors can look purple; purple is not a fourth oscillator.

Combined means curves overlaid on one 0–100 scale—not a blended score. %D is calculated but not drawn in this preview.

How the three calculations differ
IndicatorWhat it measuresOur review settings
RSISmoothed gains relative to smoothed losses.14-period Wilder smoothing; 0–100. Recursive memory extends beyond 14 bars.
Williams %RClose within the rolling highest-high / lowest-low range.14 bars; −100 to 0 natively. Add 100 only for the shared display scale.
Stochastic %K / %DThe same raw range position, then smoothed.14-bar range → SMA3 gives %K → another SMA3 gives %D.

If the range is 90–110 and the close is 106, raw Stochastic is 80 and Williams is −20. Their displayed curves differ because %K is smoothed. RSI cannot be obtained from those three prices alone.

Fourteen 3H bars are nominally 42 bar-hours; fourteen 4H bars are 56. Session-truncated bars and market closures matter. These are comparison horizons, not universal quality thresholds or equivalent indicator memory.

Sources: TradingView RSI · Williams %R · Stochastic.

Independent diagonals and historical limits

Open saved independent-fit comparison ↗. Those methods have not been merged into Trendoscope.

Retention, calculation lookback and object limits are separate. Preserve emitted channels before retirement; do not stretch one present-day channel backward and call it historical detection. Increasing retained patterns may also alter overlap logic, so capture and replay must verify that behavior.

Comparing 1H, 3H and 4H without changing the viewing window

Use the same ticker, session, adjustment settings and common date window. Detect separately on each source timeframe, then display captured rails over the same candles and scale. Compare anchors, duration, slope in a common basis, boundary distance and confirmation time—not screenshot angles.

There is no fixed “RSI14-equivalent” timeframe for a variable-length zigzag pattern. Measure actual durations across these three sources, then select useful coverage. Agreement across correlated timeframes is a hypothesis to test, not automatically independent evidence.
