No table. Just the name and its geiger. The left column is always ranked by geiger — that is the reference. Pick another column on the right and every name is joined to itself by a line. Flat lines mean the two orderings agree. Lines that cross the whole way mean that column is telling you something the geiger is not.

What you are looking for. A name whose line runs almost flat is one the geiger and that column already agree on — the column is largely inside the geiger. A name whose line dives from the top of one side to the bottom of the other is the interesting one: strong on one measure, weak on the other. If nearly every line is flat, that column is telling you nothing new. If they all cross, the two measures are close to unrelated.
