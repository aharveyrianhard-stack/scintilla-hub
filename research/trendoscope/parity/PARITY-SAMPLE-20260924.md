## Part 3 — what a faithful replay still needs from you

1. The source of the six Trendoscope Pine libraries the fork imports (see MISSING-DETECTOR-SOURCES.md).
2. A detector-side export, per accepted pattern, at the bar `valid` first becomes true:
   `patternName`, `zigzag lane` and recursion `level`, the ordered 5/6 pivots with
   `{side, source bar index, epoch, price, is_synthetic}`, and both rails' `p1`/`p2` index, epoch and price.
3. Confirmation that the saved instance is still V2 (`Kqm3pV`) and that retention is 20 with Avoid Overlap on.

Without (1) and (2) the event packet in `research/trendoscope/lib/event.mjs` has a schema but no events.
