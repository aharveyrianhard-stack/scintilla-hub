What the words mean

a day · a finished daily bar from the chart API; today's unfinished session is never counted.
prior observations only · the window holds the values BEFORE the reading being placed; the reading is never inside the sample it is compared with.
percentile · the share of prior days with a lower value (ties count half). 90 means nine prior days in ten were lower.
Z · how many standard deviations today sits from the window's mean; sd is the sample kind (n−1). Undefined when every prior value is identical, and it says so.
RSI(14) · Wilder's, RMA smoothing, exactly as the rulebook's I-8 states it (RSI = 100 when the average loss is 0). distance to the 200-day · 100 × (close ÷ 200-day simple average − 1). usual day · the standard deviation of the last 60 daily % moves, the board's heartbeat number.
the rulebook's lines · RSI 30/70 (Wilder's fixed defaults, I-8) and the 10th/90th percentile lines of the window (the rulebook's PERCENTILE mode, U-3). The 200-day itself is a rulebook object (I-4); the ±10% and ±20% distance lines are reference lines chosen for this page, not the book's. The usual day has no rulebook line.
short history · fewer prior sessions than the window asks for; too few · fewer than 30 valid prior values. Both are shown, flagged, never hidden.
