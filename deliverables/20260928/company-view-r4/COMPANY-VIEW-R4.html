2 · Earnings per ticker — the strip

The company's EVENTS tab now opens with a strip of its reports, oldest on the left and newest on the right, plus the next report as a dashed card with estimates only. Each metric is judged against its own analysts' estimate:

WordMeans
DOUBLE BEATEPS and revenue both beat
BEATone beat, the other in line (or not stored)
INLINEboth within ±1% of the estimate
MIXEDone beat, one missed
MISSone missed, the other in line (or not stored)
DOUBLE MISSboth missed

A beat is more than 1% above the estimate and a miss is more than 1% below: surprise = (actual − estimate) ÷ |estimate|. The ±1% band is our setting, not a market rule, and the legend on the page says so (see decision 2). Transcripts: we hold the full text of calls from FMP (earnings_call_transcripts: 1,406 calls, 8 of MU's last 16 reports, 9 of NVDA's). A card whose call we hold carries ◹ transcript, which opens it in the Hub's own reader. No transcript web link is stored: the url column is empty on every row. Where a press-release link is stored, the card carries ◹ release.

4 · The LIKED list — sort by the day's % or by Geiger

Under "LIKED · 128" on the expanded view there are two buttons, DAY % and GEIGER. DAY % sorts by today's change against yesterday's close, biggest gain first. GEIGER sorts by the Geiger reading, strongest first. A name with no value goes last. The slim mini Geiger stays under every name. Your choice is remembered in this browser, and ↑/↓ walks the list in the order it shows.

Where each number comes from

  • Social counts: youtube_videos (source, subscription_accounts, title, ticker) and the X feed (/api/xfeed, the SENTIMENT room's shared cache). Leans: youtube_video_sentiment where stored, otherwise the SENTIMENT room's word list run in the browser.
  • Earnings strip: earnings_events (the actual and estimated EPS and revenue, the report time, the release link), and the stored call dates from earnings_call_transcripts. Nothing new is read: this is the same data the EVENTS tab already loads, now up to 24 rows.
  • LIKED list: the board's own rows (CHG = change against yesterday's close; Geiger = the board's reading).
  • Financials comparison: the Massive and FMP APIs, called once from the Fly bar service. Nothing was stored.

What could be wrong

  • "Your channels" means any of your five YouTube accounts, including golf and soundscapes. A search-found video from a channel you also subscribe to counts as yours only when that channel shows up as a subscription in the same read.
  • Word-list leans are rough. "Record-breaking" can count as bearish ("breaking"), and a title says little. A lean with ·p is worked out fresh in each browser and stored nowhere.
  • The X feed has been stale since Fri 25 Sep 18:29 ET. Its counts and leans stop there until the collector publishes again.
  • The ±1% INLINE band is our choice. A 1-cent EPS beat on a $2 estimate (0.5%) is called INLINE here, while the Hub's earnings room calls it a beat.
  • The phone board drops F P/E, MKT CAP, REVENUE, TREND, MOM, READ and RVOL. They come back on any screen wider than 560 px.
