Live since 28 Sep 2026, 16:22 UTC. Polymarket is read every 15 minutes in US hours (New York weekdays 08:00–17:59) and every hour otherwise, into a new table. Kalshi is mapped but switched off, because Kalshi's API terms appear to limit its data to a member's own trading; that is your decision (below). The page itself is on the branch, not yet on the Hub; the coordinator deploys it.

What the page shows

  • 26 topics in 8 groups: the Fed (the next three meetings, number of hikes, number of cuts), yields (10-year to year-end, 2-year end of September), stocks (S&P year-end, SPY this month), recession (2026, 2027), Iran (both ceasefires, the blockade, invasion, nuclear deal), Russia–Ukraine (ceasefire, peace talks, NATO clash, Putin out), tariffs and trade (China tariff, new trade deals, effective tariff rate), and the midterms (House, Senate, balance of power).
  • For each outcome: the chance now on each venue, side by side; the change over 1 day and 1 week in percentage points; and a small 7-day line, green if up on the day, red if down.
  • Flashing now: every outcome whose chance moved at least N points in a day, up or down — your scintillation idea. N starts at 10 and you can change it on the page.
  • What the busiest markets are: the 15 most-traded Polymarket markets of the last 24 hours that we do not already track, with sports and games removed — the "most-mentioned topics" list, for spotting the next topic.

Where each number comes from

  • Polymarket chance: the "Yes" price Polymarket publishes for each outcome (the middle of its best offers, or the last trade when the offers are far apart). Open interest from Polymarket's Data API.
  • Kalshi chance (while switched off): the older 6-hourly reader (catalyst_odds, running since 24 Sep) — only the Fed and recession topics, and only the four most-held Fed outcomes. Cells it does not cover say "not read".
  • History: our own readings from 28 Sep 16:22 UTC on; before that, for Polymarket, Polymarket's own hourly price history for the 8 days before, loaded once so the 1-week change works today.
  • Topic list: supabase/functions/prediction-markets/topics.json, a plain file with each topic's exact market ids on each venue, its wording, and the rows lined up between venues. Edit it and redeploy to add or change a topic.

What could be wrong

  • A market's price is only as good as its trading. Thin outcomes (under 1%, or with wide offers) can sit at a stale price; that is why tails under 1% are left out unless they line up with the other venue.
  • "Not the same question" topics (yields, S&P, China tariff) are worded differently on each venue. The page says so on the card; the numbers must not be subtracted.
  • Some rows are self-describing only with the card's text: on the 10-year card a bare Polymarket "5.2%" means "touches 5.2% before 2027" and "Below 4.7%" means "touches below 4.7%".
  • Fixed ids go out of date as markets settle: the 2-year (30 Sep), SPY this month (1 Oct) and the October Fed meeting (28 Oct). The collector reports a closed event as a problem on the run row; the topic list then needs the next id.
  • The week of history before 28 Sep is Polymarket's hourly record, not our reading; a move inside an hour will not show there.
