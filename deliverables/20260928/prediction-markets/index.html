What each number means

Chance (the big number)
The market price of a "yes" share, which pays $1 if the outcome happens. A price of 71¢ reads as a 71% chance. On Polymarket it is the middle of the best buy and sell offers (or the last trade when those are far apart); on Kalshi it is the middle of a tight bid and ask, else the last trade.
1 day, 1 week
How many percentage points the chance moved since the same time yesterday and a week ago. +8.0 means it went from, say, 60% to 68%. "—" means we have no reading that far back yet.
The small line
The chance over the last 7 days, same 0–100% scale in every cell, green if it is up on the day and red if down. It is a step line: it holds each value until the next reading that differed.
✦ Flash
Alan's scintillation: an outcome whose chance moved at least the number of points you set above within one day, up or down. The default comes from the topic list (10 points).
Market expiry
Every market has a last day, after which it stops trading and pays out; when a market we track reaches it, the page moves that topic to the next market of the same kind by itself (after the 28 Oct Fed meeting, the December meeting), and its line starts again from the new market's first reading.
"Not the same question"
Some topics are asked differently on each venue (for example, where the S&P closes on 31 Dec versus which levels it touches before then). Read those side by side; do not subtract one from the other.
Where the history comes from
Everything from our first live read (28 Sep 2026, 16:22 UTC) onward is our own reading. The week before that, for Polymarket, is Polymarket's own hourly price history, loaded once so the 1-week change works from day one. Kalshi numbers come from the older 6-hourly catalyst-odds reader until Kalshi is switched on here.
