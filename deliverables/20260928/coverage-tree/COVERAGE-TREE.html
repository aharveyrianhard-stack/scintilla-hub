2 · COVERAGE BY FUND

Every fund we serve or draw on the map that has a holdings file. Served is how many of its holdings the Hub carries (in the chart API's /universe). Coverage is the share of the fund's weight those names are. Top 10 says how many of the fund's ten heaviest lines we serve. Weight per name is coverage ÷ served names: low means many names carrying little weight (over-covered by count). Tracks is the correlation of the fund's own daily Geiger with the weight-blend of our names' daily Geigers over the last 500 sessions (section 4 explains). Smallest set is how few of our names reach that same correlation. Click a row for its names and its misses. Click a column title to sort.

3 · THE TREE, GEIGER BASED

The market at the top, then regions and asset classes, then the eleven sectors, then each sector's funds (the State Street fund, its iShares, Vanguard and equal-weight twins, and the industry and theme funds), then Alan's cohorts under the fund that is their ETF parent, then the names. Every line carries two bars: solid = its own live Geiger; striped = the aggregate of what sits under it (a fund: its served holdings blended by weight; a cohort: the plain mean of its members; a heading: the mean of the lines directly under it). A cohort with no fund holding at least a third of its members sits under COHORTS WITH NO ETF PARENT. Click ▸ to open a branch.
