3 · Where each number comes from

What you seeSource
Timeline bars (earnings count per day, week or month)earnings_events (ticker and date only), read for what is on screen plus a page either side. Unchanged maths: the colour is the count, and the height is scaled to the busiest bar on screen.
TODAY lineThe page's own New York date (todayISO), placed inside the bar that holds it.
History bars and past days in USUAL DAYsigma_day_counts and sigma_events_daily (new, see below), computed from the chart API's daily bars (/candles?tf=1d, split-adjusted, back to 11 Sep 2003).
A sigma dayThe rules file (data/scintilla-rules.json, version 2026-09-24.1), the same test the live detector uses. Move = close ÷ previous close − 1. Usual day = the spread of the 60 daily moves before that day, with at least 20 moves needed. A day counts if it is at least 2× its usual day and at least 1% (equities), or over the plain floor (8% for equities, 4% for sector funds, and so on).
Today's countThe live detector's store (scintillas), price moves only. Up and down count moves, not earnings or economic surprises.
Percentile1 to 100: the share of earlier sessions at or below today's value, compared on the share of names measured, because fewer names were measured in 2003 (25 on the first day, 350 on a typical day, 486 now). Shown for ALL names only.
Today's MOVE vs PREV CLOSEThe board's live price ÷ the served previous close (live_quotes.prev_close) − 1. Where there is none, the move when the detector first saw it, labelled "at first sight".
⊙ ★ ♥ marks and chipsYour RADAR, FAVORITES and LIKED lists, the same ones the dashboard uses.

5 · What could be wrong

  • Today and history are measured almost, but not exactly, the same way. History uses each day's close. Today's count comes from the live detector, which counts a name when it first crosses the line during the day. A name that recovers stays counted until the close. Today's percentile is provisional until the backfill's nightly run measures the closed day.
  • Days between the last stored session and today fall back to the live detector's store. That store only exists from 23 Sep, so a missed nightly run shows those days from the detector instead.
  • The reused-ticker cut also cuts a genuine trading halt longer than 20 days. 59 names were cut; I did not check each one by hand.
  • Bars are split-adjusted but not dividend-adjusted. A large special dividend can show as a down move.
  • A list's history is read up to 5,000 rows per 60 names. A very large list over 250 sessions could be cut short. LIKED, FAVORITES and RADAR are far below that.
  • The local proof answered the two new tables' reads from the dry-run file, because the tables do not exist yet. Every other read went to the live Supabase and the live chart API. All writes were stubbed and nothing was sent.
  • The bell list and the ⛶ panel sit inside a part of the page that is drawn scaled at wide screens. Both are now placed by measuring where they actually land. I checked this at 1680 and 390 only.
  • During some shots the header said STALE or CONNECTING. That is the live feed's own state at that moment and is unrelated to this work.
