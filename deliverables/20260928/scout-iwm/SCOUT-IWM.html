4 · In plain words

What this page shows. Every stock inside IWM (the iShares Russell 2000 small-cap fund), with the few numbers you need to decide whether a name deserves a closer look — without adding it to the Hub. Section 1 is a screen you can change. Section 2 looks only at the Russell 2000 names the Hub already carries. Section 3 shows where the screen's survivors come from. Nothing here says buy or sell.

Where each number comes from. Holdings and weights: FMP ETF holdings for IWM (dated in the header). Prices: every US stock's daily bar, read in one piece per trading day — our own copy of Massive's whole-market day files (to 17 Aug 2026) and Massive's grouped-daily call after that — adjusted for splits. From those: RSI 14 (the standard Wilder RSI on daily closes); RSI percentile = where today's RSI sits among every daily RSI that stock has printed since June 2023 (0 = its lowest, 100 = its highest); vs 50-day / vs 200-day = how far the close is above or below its 50- and 200-day average; from 1-yr high = how far below the highest price of the last 252 trading days; 3-mo vs IWM = the stock's last-63-day return against IWM's over the same days (+10% = it beat IWM by 10%); $ traded / day = average of price × shares over the last 20 days. Company numbers: FMP bulk files (one download for all companies) — market value, trailing-twelve-month earnings and free cash flow, P/E, debt ÷ equity. Next report: FMP earnings calendar. Geiger (section 2): the live Geiger the Hub already shows (-1 to +1).

What could be wrong. FMP's holdings file can lag the fund by days. Company numbers are trailing twelve months and can be a quarter old. Debt ÷ equity is misleading for banks and for companies with negative equity (shown as a blank or an extreme). A stock with less than about a year of trading has no RSI percentile (blank). "Today" is Massive's snapshot at the time in the header — before 09:30 it is yesterday's move. A ticker spelled differently at FMP and Massive can miss its prices (listed as blank rows at the bottom of "all holdings").

What was not done. No name was added to the Hub, the database, or the Geiger. Nothing here is refreshed automatically yet — this is one run on 28 Sep. The page does not rank "best" names; the order is only the column you choose.
