In short

  • A click on RVol does rank the board by the number beside the battery: FAVORITES and ALL, high → low on the first click, low → high on the second, names with no number at the bottom both ways. The order holds through the 45 s pulls, and it is the same on seven screen sizes (section 3, 6).
  • The REWIND bar just above the board undid it. Sorted by RVol, any touch of the rewind (a drag, ▶ PLAY, the compare's Replay) and then the way back to live (↻ LIVE, Esc, the 45 s idle return, the end of a replay) left the live board in Geiger order under "RVol ▼" until the next 45 s pull: 33 neighbouring numbers stepping the wrong way and 64 numbers below a dash. The header says RVol, the rows move, but not by volume.
  • The fix: the replay still ranks by the Geiger alone while the board is rewound (Alan, 24 Sep: "The Geiger replay is with the Geiger bar only"); back to live, the board ranks by its own column again, as that note promised. One branch in one function of the rewind; nothing else changes.

sortBoardBy ranks by RVol. The rewind's orderPass then ranks by g twice: rewound (by design) and again from goLive → repaint, after the rewind has already ended. S.sort was still RVol ▼ the whole time, so the header kept saying RVol. The 45 s pull (mergeBoardRows) put the RVol order back.

The code. goLive() clears ASOF and then calls repaint(), whose orderPass() forced the Geiger for every repaint (23 Sep, commit 158a21e, whose own note said "the board's own sort is left untouched, so LIVE ranks exactly as it did before PLAY"):
