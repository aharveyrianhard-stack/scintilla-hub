7 · collector hours (not applied) and summaries

The sweep earnings-sweep-generic (cron job 182) runs */10 10-16,20-23,0-4 * * * UTC — nothing 05:00–09:59 UTC (01:00–05:59 ET), which is exactly when TSMC (13:30 Taipei = 01:30 ET) and ASML (07:00 CEST = 01:00 ET) publish. The SQL and its rollback are in cron-earnings-sweep-hours.sql. The cron alone is not enough: the deployed function gates BMO rows to 06:00–11:59 ET (its windowOpen); the one-function change that goes with the schedule is in earnings-sweep-window.patch.md. Neither was applied.

Who wrote the Q2 summaries. The edge function transcript-summarize (v4, 21 Jul) — a POST to api.anthropic.com with the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY secret, model claude-sonnet-4-6 by default — and earnings-summarize (v6, 22 Jul) for the event summaries. Both were called by the sweep until v12 (18 Sep) made the sweep model-free; the last batch of call summaries is dated 18 Sep 11:20 UTC (FedEx, 8 quarters). That is why this quarter's 14 calls have no ai_summary. Without a paid model API, this quarter's calls could be summarised by a worker on this Mac that reads the rows where ai_summary is null (the sweep already lists them in app_config.earnings_sweep_result.summary_needed), runs the summary through the Claude Max subscription (Claude Code non-interactively, no API key), and writes ai_summary through a sanctioned write — an approval and a write route the coordinator would have to grant; no local model is installed on this Mac (no Ollama, no LM Studio). Nothing of that was built.

  • (1) card placement — ercDayHTML draws the picked card between the day's header and its groups; ercWeekHTML draws it above its day; ercShowPick() scrolls it into view inside its square after a chip click.
  • (2) times — ernWhenFull(r): "before the open" / "after the close" / "09:00 ET" for a US name; "before the US open · Taipei, 12 h ahead" or "01:30 ET (13:30 Taipei)" for a name whose company_profile.country is abroad (one read of company_profile?select=ticker,country,exchange,ir_url, kept for the session; 179 of 613 profiles have no country and read as US); "time not announced · date confirmed / date not confirmed" when nothing is stored. Zone arithmetic from each zone's own calendar (ernTzAhead), so daylight saving on both sides is respected. ernSlot: a stored clock before 09:30 ET = before the open, 16:00 or later = after the close, between = during the session. Only non-empty groups are drawn; the day's cut is printed in one line.
  • (4) honest buttons — ernCallLabel: a newswire host (businesswire, prnewswire, globenewswire, accesswire, newsfilecorp) reads "Call notice · host" with the truth in the title; any other host "Call · host". ernIrUrl: "IR page · host" from company_profile.ir_url.
