WHAT THE TRIAL PAGE DOES

  • The title is the unit. 22 px / 18 px, weight 600, two lines, white on the Hub's own black. Under it, in the mono: source · age · ticker chips. A chip is every name the story was filed under (wire echoes of one story under five tickers are folded into one line with five chips) plus any cashtag or universe name standing in the title. A chip opens the company: /#co=NVDA, a 7-line addition to index.html on this branch (the Hub had no way to open a company by address).
  • Grouped by freshness: LAST HOUR (green, pulsing), TODAY (cyan), YESTERDAY (violet), EARLIER. Today and yesterday are two pulls, each windowed on published_ts and capped on its own, so a results day (TGT: 210 headlines today) cannot push yesterday off the end.
  • Scopes are the Hub's: ⊙ RADAR, ★ FAVORITES, ♥ LIKED (station_lists and hub_favorites, read the way the Hub reads them), ALL (the only scope that shows the market-wide rows), then the twelve cohorts by ticker_cohorts membership. The scope is remembered per browser; ?scope= and #TICKER address it.
  • What moved · today: every ticker-tagged headline since midnight ET, counted per name (6,600+ rows on 15 Oct, read 1,000 at a time), the twelve busiest with a bar, the count, and the name's newest title; green at 100 or more; a tap shows that name. Names outside the current scope are dimmed, not hidden — that is the rail's point.
  • Press releases (prnewswire, globenewswire, businesswire, accesswire, newsfile) get a yellow edge and a PR tag, so an issuer's own words are told apart from a reporter's.
  • Notifications · this device: the one-click Allow (below). The card says plainly when a browser cannot do it (iPhone not on the Home Screen; notifications blocked in site settings) instead of a dead button.
  • It writes nothing — except, on Alan's own click, one row to push_subscriptions.

PAGE SPECS · the push pieces
  • push-sw.js (root) — push → showNotification(title, body, tag = the event key so a repeat replaces, icon /icon-192.png, data.url); notificationclick → focus an open Hub window and navigate it, else open one. No fetch handler, no caches. Registered by the page with scope: "/push/".
  • index.html (branch) — FIX 7b's clearing block skips a registration whose scope ends in /push/; /#co=TICKER opens the company on boot and on hashchange. The trial copy at /preview/company-view/ was rebuilt by build-trial.py, as the house rule says.
  • supabase/functions/notify-push/index.ts + webpush.mjs — GET ?mode=vapid; POST {title, body, url, key, kind}; ?mode=events; ?mode=test. Each send: fresh ephemeral P-256 pair, 16-byte salt, aes128gcm, TTL 3600, urgency high, topic = kind. 404/410 prune the row; other failures are recorded on it. Every call writes a push_log row (sent / failed / declined with reason).
  • supabase/migrations/20261015_push_subscriptions.sql — push_subscriptions (RLS: anon INSERT with shape checks, anon DELETE by endpoint, no anon SELECT), push_log (service role only; unique sent key), push_quiet_now(), push_can_send_now(), news_usual_per_hour(t) = 14-day count / 336, news_bursts_now() (≥3 in the hour and ≥ 3 × max(usual, 1)), push_events_pending() — the three sentences, de-duplicated against push_log, newest first. Rollback: …_ROLLBACK.sql.
  • supabase/migrations/20261015_push_ntfy_OPTIONAL.sql — route (b1) as a function over pg_net with the topic in Vault; the cron line to wire it, commented out.
