5 · THE TWO RULES ON THE TAB

Never a silent dash

A row with no value prints not stored in the dim grey of the "as of" notes, and its hover says which table and column is empty. A live reading that is not there says no reading. A loss-maker's P/E says none · loss. A name FMP lists no dividend for says none on record. The one thing H7 did right is kept: a block with no value at all (a fund's GROWTH) is not drawn — not a column of "not stored" either.

A fund has no earnings

Any name the page already treats as non-operating (company_profile.is_etf, or a crypto name) gets no "next report" / "last report" rows, no VALUE, GROWTH or BALANCE block, no revenue row, no float row — and lists its own five facts every time, "not stored" where etf_info lacks one. Checked in the walk: SPY, QQQ, IWM, SMH, XLK. One trap avoided: FMP also flags O (Realty Income) as a fund (is_fund = true) — it is a REIT that reports every quarter, so that flag is read but never used as the gate.

6 · WHAT COULD BE WRONG, OR LOOKS ODD

  • "today" after the close. The probe found the stored quote row carries no change figure on all 50 names after 16:00 ET, while the header showed MU −2.15%. The tab now falls back to the board row's reading and says so on hover. Why the quote row loses its change after the close was not chased here.
  • The dividend yield is FMP's at the ex-date, not today's price. The year's dividends paid against today's market cap (the older row, from cashflow_history) stays beside it for companies.
  • The facts are a snapshot of 2 Oct. Nothing refreshes them yet; shares and dividends move. The hover on every fact row carries "facts as of Oct 2 2026 (FMP)" so a stale number is never mistaken for a fresh one.
  • The seven names without a profile row (MOG.A and the six treasury-yield series) still get the tab's "No stats inputs loaded" line; nothing on this branch fills them.
  • Not verified by hand on your screen — all proof is headless, from this branch served locally against the live data.
