00 · THE SHORT VERSION

Alan asked: "What's the next price tier or the extra provider that would give us amazing analyst estimates for financials — broken down as best possible, with good detail? How much would that cost us?"

  • FMP's own higher tiers do not help. Every FMP plan has the same estimate fields (revenue, EBITDA, EBIT, net income, SG&A, EPS). Paying FMP more buys speed, bulk files and transcripts, not better estimates.
  • The "history by analyst, going back years" Alan wants exists at retail price only for price targets and ratings: Benzinga's Analyst Ratings through our existing Massive account, $99 a month. It has each analyst's rating and target, the one before it, and an analyst ID, back to 2012.
  • The best retail upgrade for the DCF is Finnhub "Estimate-2", $200 a month for US stocks (billed yearly). It adds consensus for capex, free cash flow, operating cash flow, gross profit and pre-tax income, five years out. These are the inputs FMP lacks, and capex is the one our DCF guesses today.
  • Each analyst's own EPS and revenue forecasts over time, and segment-level consensus (for example Nvidia data-centre revenue for 2028), come only from the institutional houses: LSEG I/B/E/S, FactSet, S&P Capital IQ / Visible Alpha and Bloomberg. None of them publishes a price. All are quote only.
  • One free step comes first: save FMP's estimates every night from now on. That starts our own revision history at no cost and shows whether NVIDIA's earnings estimates are rising while its targets fall.

ESTIMATES tab — "revisions, by analyst, going back"

  • Targets and ratings by analyst, back to 2012: Benzinga on Massive, $99/mo. FMP already gives the newest of these with the analyst's name (R1). Benzinga adds the stable analyst ID, the previous target on every row, and the deep history needed for a "track record per analyst" chart.
  • Are earnings estimates going up while targets go down? (the NVIDIA question): nightly snapshots of FMP's estimates answer it from tonight onward at no cost. EODHD's Earnings Trend answers it today for the last 90 days ($59.99/mo).
  • Each analyst's own EPS forecast, years back: only I/B/E/S, FactSet, Capital IQ or Bloomberg. Quote only.

DCF — "honest inputs"

  • Capex, free cash flow and operating cash flow consensus, 5 years out: Finnhub Estimate-2, $200/mo (US). This replaces our "capex as a share of past revenue" guess with what analysts expect.
  • Gross and pre-tax margin forecasts: also Finnhub Estimate-2. EBITDA, EBIT and net-income margins we already have from FMP.
  • Segment forecasts (data centre versus gaming, DRAM versus NAND): only Visible Alpha / Capital IQ, FactSet or Bloomberg. Quote only. Segment history we already have from FMP's product and geographic segments.

04 · RECOMMENDATION

In plain words: no single retail plan is "amazing" on every front. The cheap, honest path is FMP plus two small add-ons. Only go institutional if you decide you need each analyst's own earnings forecasts over the years.
  1. Tonight, free: start saving FMP's estimates and price-target news every night (the "smallest build" from R1, plus an estimates snapshot). From the first night we have our own revision history, including the NVIDIA "estimates up, targets down" check.
  2. Cheapest buy with the biggest gain: the Benzinga Analyst Ratings dataset on Massive, $99 a month. Same vendor and same key we already use. It answers "do we have a history by analyst, can we go back" for targets and ratings since 2012. It gives the ESTIMATES tab its revision strip and a per-analyst visual.
  3. Second, when the DCF rebuild starts: Finnhub Estimate-2 (US), $200 a month billed yearly = $2,400 a year. It adds capex, FCF, operating cash flow and gross / pre-tax margins five years out.
  4. Keep from FMP: annual and quarterly consensus (revenue, EBITDA, EBIT, net income, SG&A, EPS, low/avg/high, analyst counts), price-target news and summary, grades, segment and geographic actuals, statements, treasury rates. Drop: FMP's DCF numbers and its beta (R1 showed why). Paying for a higher FMP tier is not needed for estimates.

Total for steps 2 and 3: $99 + $200 = about $299 a month list price, if both are bought. Neither is bought here.

05 · DECISIONS FOR ALAN

1 · Buy Benzinga Analyst Ratings on Massive ($99/mo)? Recommendation: yes, first. This is where "history by analyst going back years" comes from for targets and ratings.
2 · Buy Finnhub Estimate-2 for US stocks ($200/mo, billed yearly) now or later? Recommendation: later, when the DCF is being rebuilt. Before paying, check its sample answers on Finnhub's documentation page for one name (for example MU capex).
3 · Who sees the Hub? Every retail plan here is personal or individual use. FMP explicitly requires a display agreement for showing its data. If the Hub stays for you alone, the personal plans fit. If anyone else will see these numbers, we need commercial quotes instead. Recommendation: keep it personal for now, and ask for institutional quotes (I/B/E/S or Visible Alpha) only if analyst-by-analyst EPS history becomes a must.
