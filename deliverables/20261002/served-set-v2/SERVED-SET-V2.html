The served set, redone Alan's way — a proposal with measurements

U3 · 2 Oct 2026 · built 2026-10-03T01:51:15.852Z · PROPOSED: nothing is admitted or removed, no table, universe, Hub page or job is touched. The coordinator runs any admission at a closed-market sitting with Alan's word.

The rule in one paragraph. For a fund that weights by size (the sector SPDRs, QQQ, SMH, IGV, …) we add its names biggest first and, at every count, ask two questions: does the Geiger blend of those names sit within ±0.1 of the blend of all its names (on the close reading and on 90% of 12 other draws), and do their daily moves explain 90% of the fund's own daily moves over 60 sessions? The first count from which both stay true is the coverage point; the share of the fund's weight at that count is the statistically relevant share — measured, not chosen. The same test at a looser live tolerance (±0.2, 80%) gives the Hub point: those names are read live on the Hub, the rest of the covered set is blended off-Hub at the close. Equal-weight funds (RSP, QQEW, the SPDR industry funds) and IWM get no cap rule: we measure how much of each the chosen names already cover, then add only names that live in other funds too, most other homes first, until the blend tracks the fund with at least 30% of its weight in or ten more names would add under one point. RADAR / FAVORITES / LIKED are always on the Hub. Cohorts are not a rule. No cap per fund, no names-per-fund headline.

6 · Where every number comes from

  • Fund holdings and weights: the FMP holdings file of 2026-09-26 the tree uses (51 of 138 tree funds). No fund was fetched anew.
  • Geiger readings: the seven-rung close run as of 2026-10-01 (main reading and its seven rungs) and the daily three-rung closes of 2026-09-25, 2026-09-28, 2026-09-29, 2026-09-30, 2026-10-01 — 12 draws in all.
  • Daily closes: the chart API, 472 symbols, 2026-06-25 → 2026-10-02; it serves the tracked universe only, so 2721 requested symbols had none.
  • The lists: LIKED (hub_favorites, 141), FAVORITES (57) and RADAR (17) from station_lists, read-only. Today's universe: the chart API, 590.
  • Nothing was written: no table, no universe, no Hub page, no job. Headless screenshots only.

7 · What could be wrong

  1. One seven-rung close exists (the 1 Oct close); the 12 draws are its seven rungs and five daily three-rung closes — they are not twelve days.
  2. Holdings exist for 51 of the 138 tree funds (FMP, 26 Sep). The other 87 cannot be measured; their names are not judged.
  3. Daily closes came from the chart API (472 symbols, 2026-06-25 → 2026-10-02; 2721 symbols had none). The returns test runs on the top 200 of each cap-weighted fund; beyond 200 only the Geiger test speaks.
  4. Alan's words were 'the Geiger of the blend vs the fund's own'. Measured literally, the fund's own Geiger (read on the fund's price) sits far from the blend of ALL its names on 50 of 51 funds (XLK: fund +0.86, all-names blend +1.28 → gap −0.43; while the same names explain 99% of XLK's daily moves). A fund's price moves smoother than its names, so its Geiger is a different quantity. The Geiger side therefore measures top-k against the all-names blend; the fund's own price is the yardstick on the returns side, which is where 'does the blend track the fund' is answered.
  5. XLB's returns fit is negative even with all 25 names in; its candle series from the chart API ends near $49 and may be another instrument's. Its Geiger side is clean; the fund went to the overlap rule on the returns side and should be re-checked once the series is confirmed.
  6. The equal-weight funds are mostly judged on the Geiger side alone (their names have no daily bars yet). One Fly job (fmp-batch-eod.mjs beside this page) fetches the missing closes from FMP's batch end-of-day route so the returns test can speak for them too.
  7. Readings are one close (1 Oct). The points will move a little from day to day; the rule is meant to be re-measured nightly and a name should change side only after it has held for several closes (U2's guard).
