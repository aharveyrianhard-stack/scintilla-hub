What this page shows

Every name the Hub serves today (590: 524 with the full treatment, 66 Geiger-only) checked on 31 surfaces: the board row and its columns, the chart's 24 widths, the Geiger rungs and previous close, every company tab (GEIGER, FUNDAMENTALS, ESTIMATES, COMPS, FINANCIALS with CAPITAL, STATS, NEWS, SOCIAL, EARNINGS, the four READ sections and the dossier's date), the EARNINGS calendar, the NEWS room and ALERTS. Each cell is OK, EMPTY, PLACEHOLDER (developer text or a stub) or ERROR (stale or wrong), with the reason and the job that fills it.

Where each number comes from

  • The checks: control/ADMISSION_CHECKLIST.json and scripts/admission-checklist.mjs in the provider repo (branch provider/f1-full-treatment-20261003). It reads the chart API (/universe, /quotes, /geiger, /candles) and the Hub database through the page's own public read endpoint — the same rows and rules the page gets — plus the Hub's X feed. GET only; no key.
  • The 24 widths for all names: U4b's R2 walk of 3 Oct 14:35Z (taking 14,160 chart reads from the live service would have loaded every series into its cache).
  • COMPS: the tab's own live rule (C4) run over today's rows.
  • The real page: 83 names opened headless on the deployed scintillahub.ai (all 56 bookmark names, 12 of the 29 Sep names, 10 original names, 4 Geiger-only funds, MOG.A), every tab and READ section clicked; 0 write requests (all blocked). The page and the data checks agree on every one of the 17 surfaces the page shows, for every sampled name (0 disagreements after aligning the checks to the page's own words).
  • The fills: FMP read on a throw-away batch machine (428 calls, all answered), turned into additive loads under supabase/migrations/20261003_f1_*, each with its rollback beside it.
  • After = projected with the same checks (--overlay data/staged-20261003.json); the page previews serve the staged row to the deployed page for one read and change nothing.

What could be wrong

  • The after column is a projection; the loads have not run. The real after is one command: the checklist with --all once they are applied.
  • The facts dossier states what is stored. Where a stored number is old (MOG.A's 2024 targets) the dossier repeats it, dated.
  • A non-USD reporter's estimates are given as dates only — the currency of FMP's estimate rows cannot be proven from the row.
  • The dossier for a fund lists its largest holdings' report dates only when those holdings are Hub names with a stored date.

What was not done

  • No table was written, no function deployed, no schedule created, no machine that already existed touched. The loads, the statements trigger, the seven-width pass and the two new schedules wait for the coordinator.
  • Geiger-only funds were not filled (their tier skips the loaders by design and Alan has asked for them off the Hub).
  • No page text or layout changed (the developer words on screen are listed, not removed).
