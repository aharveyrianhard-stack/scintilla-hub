A throwaway page, one part per section, inside out. Every "A · today" is the live tree page (origin/hub/release-20260923 @9d2b1ca) as it drew itself in a headless Chrome at 1920 × 1080 (the Apple TV, scale 1) on 3 Oct 2026 with Alan's real lists planted in the Hub mirror: a 1:1 window on the real screenshot wherever one is shown (its title says the file and the pixel box), or the page's own markup redrawn to the measured numbers where the real thing is a table or a card. Every "B" and "C" is a drawing in the Hub's look — the company tabs GEIGER / FUNDAMENTALS are the reference: plain, uppercase, no boxes, bull #00FFA3 / bear #FF2D55 on #0A0A0F, the mono type, cyan for chrome only. The numbers beside each part are measured, not typed: the box and label rectangles, the panel rectangles, the camera distance and the black share come from shots/parts-proof-A.json and parts-proof-B.json (the run of proof-parts.mjs); the readings are today's live Hub Geiger (data/hub-geiger-20261003.json, 2026-10-03T15:16:37.439Z) and the close Geiger of 2 Oct (data/scout-geiger-20261003.json) for names off the Hub. A size is given as one share of the 1920 × 1080 screen, then the pixels. Nothing here is applied: no file of the live tree, no Hub page and no table was changed; the coil is lane V2's and is linked, not redrawn. The recommendation on each part is one option, marked, for Alan to keep, change or refuse.
