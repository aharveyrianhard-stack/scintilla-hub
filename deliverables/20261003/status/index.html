    Where the words come from: a read-only measurement on 3 Oct 2026, 11:00–11:30 ET — the database's own job history (pg_cron), the real answers of the web calls it makes (cron_dispatch), the newest row in each job's output table, the Fly machines and their own last log line, and the MacBook's scheduled jobs.     When it goes live: the same lines come from the job heartbeat table (one row per job, judged every 5 minutes) and from Q2a's night verdict, instead of a snapshot.

    What could be wrong: the "since" dates are the oldest the systems still remember (pg_cron keeps 14 days; web answers 14 days; raw answers 6 hours) — a fault may be older than its date here.
