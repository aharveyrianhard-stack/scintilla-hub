What this is. A throwaway lab (deliverables/20261003/coil-lab/index.html) on branch hub/v2-coil-lab-20261003. The live tree page is untouched; the Hub is untouched. Everything here is a proposal for Alan to pick from.

The coil's data. The Hub Geiger from the chart API /geiger, read live when the page opens (the snapshot of 3 Oct 14:36 UTC stands in if it does not answer, and the stamp says which). The list is ★ FAVORITES read through the Hub mirror in the browser (sc_lists), the same way the tree reads it; where the mirror is empty the same list read from the Hub's tables on 3 Oct is used (58 names, 56 with a reading; GCUSD and BTCUSD have no Hub Geiger). The geometry is T9/T10's: the spiral, tread = reading × H, one H above and below zero (H came out at 249 per unit for FAVORITES), the red half mirrored and hanging. Sizes come from the T10 sheet (data/tree-standard.t10-e133a81.json), the colours and type from the Hub's own tokens (cyan is chrome, --bull and --bear are the only readings).

Motion, and what it means. Nothing moves on its own. The readings LAND: every step grows from the zero plane to its reading, 900 ms, 28 ms apart from the centre out. A VALUE CHANGE eases the step to its new height and signals once — LASER flares the beam 320 ms, LIGHT-GUIDE runs a bead from zero to the tip 420 ms, HOLOGRAM re-materialises the plate 400 ms. REPLAY A TICK plays the real change between the 3 Oct snapshot and the live Geiger (2 names had moved when the captures were made); when nothing has changed it nudges Friday's fired names by their own direction and back. A name that fired today flickers once every 20 s in HOLOGRAM — the Hub's "soon" cadence.

How the frame rate was measured. Headless Chrome 154 launched by Playwright with --use-angle=metal, which gives the page the Mac's real GPU (the renderer reported itself as ANGLE Metal, Apple M5 Max — it is in shots/proof.json). MEASURE FPS turns the camera 90° over 4 s with every frame rendered and counts the frames; it also times the renderer's own call. All three directions held 60 fps at 1680 × 1050 at scale 2 and on the phone — 60 is the screen's cap, and the renderer took about a millisecond of each 16.7 ms frame, so there is room. Headless frame pacing is not a display; the number says the browser never had to drop a frame, not what the eye will see. A real check on the MacBook's screen is for Alan.

The tape's data. public.scintillas, the table the Hub's SCINTILLAS line reads, pulled 3 Oct through the Hub's own read path and saved beside the page. Friday 2 Oct: 12 rows, 9 up, 3 down, all price outliers stamped 21:29 ET (the detector ran once after the close). 24 Sep (33 price outliers + 1 econ) and 28 Sep (27 + 5) are the month's busiest sessions; the econ rows have no direction and are not marks. A mark's size is how many times its usual day it moved (|z|); an ETF that fired the raw 3 % rule is sized by its move at 3 % ≈ 1× and labelled with the %. Small moves keep a 3 px floor and their number, so the name always reads.

Sizes. T1 is 4 % of the screen tall (42 px at 1050; the Hub's tape height). T2 and T3 are 50 % of the screen (525 px at 1050) — the "bigger screen mode"; on the phone the ladder scrolls inside its panel at 18 px a row.

What was not done. No Hub change, no tree change, no deploy. The LIGHT-GUIDE bead and the scintilla flicker are built in one direction each, not all three. The T12a parts list had not been pushed when this ran; the template names the coil's sub-parts after T9/T10's code. The LIKED podium (144 names) was pictured but not measured. The frame rate was measured headless, not on a screen.
