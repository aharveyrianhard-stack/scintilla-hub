What this shows: twenty tree names (the FAVORITES list's first twenty, with their Hub Geiger readings from the 3 Oct snapshot) drawn the way T12's parts gallery draws part 02 — the ticker on its box, 700 weight, white, letter-spaced — but sized by label-scale.mjs instead of a fixed 15 px. The boxes sit where a zoomed tree would put them: 60 px apart at home, twice as far zoomed in, half as far zoomed out. The rule: px = 13 × (home distance ÷ distance), floor 11 px, ceiling 24 px, on a 1080-tall screen (scaled with the screen's height). When two labels' boxes would touch (4 px of air), the weaker reading gives way; zoomed in, the boxes spread and every label comes back.

Where the numbers come from: label-scale.mjs (the same file the coil lab imports), data/geiger-snapshot-20261003.json, data/hub-lists-20261003.json. Nothing here touches the live tree; it is the demonstration the brief asked for ("you could use that for the tree too").
