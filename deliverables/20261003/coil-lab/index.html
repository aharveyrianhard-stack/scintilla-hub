Source: public.scintillas, the table the Hub's SCINTILLAS line and bell read, pulled 3 Oct 2026 through the Hub's own read path (data/scintillas-20261002.json and data/scintillas-20260901-20261003.json). Every mark is a stored row; nothing is invented. Friday 2 Oct has 12 rows (9 up, 3 down), all stamped at the close (21:29 ET), because the detector ran once after the session. 24 Sep (34 rows) and 28 Sep (32 rows) are the month's busiest sessions and stand in for "when 40 fire at once"; ALL THREE puts their 78 rows on one tape.

A mark's length is how many times its usual day the move was (|z|). A fire on the raw-move rule (an index or sector ETF moving 3 % or more) has |z| below 1, so it is sized by its move at 3 % ≈ 1×, and the label prints the % instead. A move too small to draw gets a 5 px floor so its name still reads.

T1 is the strip at the Hub's tape height: 4 % of the screen (42 px at 1050 tall). It drifts right → left at 22 px/s and can be dragged. T2 and T3 are the "bigger screen mode": a full panel (here 520 px tall ≈ 50 % of the screen at 1050). T2 only has a time axis to use when the detector fires intraday (24 and 28 Sep did; Friday did not — all twelve sit on one minute, so T2 fans them by size). T3 needs no time: forty names are forty rows of 18–26 px, all on one screen.

Pass 2 (3 Oct, afternoon): one scale for both sides of the ladder (the longest bar on either side = the biggest × usual day of the day; the down side's bars had been drawn into a 44 px column by a right-to-left grid, so ICE 8.9× was a stub while TJX 9.2× filled its row); both sides sorted by × its usual day; the × column always prints × its usual day (EWY: 0.8×, then its +3.1 % move). The bigger-screen mode: a tap on the strip (a press without a drag) opens the ladder as the whole screen — BACK, Esc or OPEN FULL PANEL's twin on T3's own box — and the strip pauses while it is open.
