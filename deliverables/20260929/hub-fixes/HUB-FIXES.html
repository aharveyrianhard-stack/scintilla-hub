4 · THE BOARD

Forward P/E blank until a refresh — the race

Every optional read the board makes has a 4-second deadline. The forward-estimate read comes in pages of 1,000 rows, one after another. When the database is slow (the page's own record shows full pulls at 17.5 s median during market hours), the deadline won, the answer that arrived a moment later was thrown away, and on a first load there was nothing older to fall back on — so every F P/E read "—" until a later pull. I replayed it by holding the estimates back 6 seconds: before, all 128 cells stayed "—" at 15 s (and TSM's tooltip wrongly said no estimate was stored); after, the late answer is kept and the cells fill where they stand (57 multiples, NVDA 19.1×). Nothing is read twice. The cells are also recomputed from the latest known price, so a name that was priced after the pull does not wait for a tick that never comes after hours.

TSM "EPS TWD"

Taiwan Semi reports its estimates in Taiwan dollars; the price is in US dollars, so the page withheld the multiple. The supplier gives both for the same quarter: TSM's Q3 26 USD EPS estimate per ADR is 4.39 and its TWD one is 139.58 — a rate of 0.03145; its revenue estimates give 0.03146. Same number, so the TWD figures are already per ADR and the ratio is a real rate. The board now converts only when the EPS ratio and the revenue ratio agree within 3%, marks it ≈, and its tooltip gives the rate, the quarter and both ratios. TSM reads ≈23.4× (614.97 TWD × 0.03145 = $19.34 NTM EPS against $452), ASML ≈32.4×. Any name whose ratios disagree keeps "EPS TWD". Revenue in TWD is still not converted.

5 · COHORT COMPARE · THE BOW TIE

COHORT COMPARE → SECTORS → BOW TIE. Each bar is the equal-weight fund's Geiger minus its cap-weight twin's: RSP − SPY, QQQE − QQQ, EQAL − IWB and the eleven RSP* sector funds against their XL* funds. Above zero, the average stock is stronger than the index; below, the index is carried by its biggest names. Sorted high to low, the bars fan out — the bow tie. The column shows the sector's short name; hovering shows the pair and both Geigers (for example "COMMS · RSPC (equal-weight) −0.67 − XLC (cap-weight) −0.19 = −0.48").

On the evening of 28 Sep: discretionary, materials and real estate +0.06; S&P 500 −0.83 (RSP −0.53 vs SPY +0.29), comms −0.48, tech −0.17, Nasdaq-100 −0.16. EQAL and IWB have no Geiger yet, so that pair says so and draws nothing.

WHAT COULD BE WRONG

  • The ≈ conversion rests on the supplier's own estimates being paired correctly by quarter (a report within 110 days after the quarter end). The 3% agreement check is the guard; a supplier change in how it states ADR EPS would make the check fail and the cell go back to "EPS TWD", not show a wrong number.
  • "After hours" is judged against 09:30–16:00 ET and the page's own NYSE holiday list; an early-close day (13:00) is not treated specially.
  • The bow tie subtracts two Geigers on the same scale; it says which basket is stronger, not by how much in returns.
  • The slow-database replay proves the fix for a late answer; I could not reproduce the blank on the real database from here (reads answered in ~140 ms).
