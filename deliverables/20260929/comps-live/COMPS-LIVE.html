What was built in one paragraph

The company view's ESTIMATES tab now carries a COMPS section (02·c), right under the analysts' price target it must never be confused with. For any served company that has a cohort, it draws the six valuation rows the way Alan approved on 28 Sep (every mark named: LOWEST PEER · 25TH · MEDIAN · 75TH · HIGHEST PEER · TICKER TODAY; multiples on top, the price each implies below; a diamond for today; the arrow to the median with the upside in % and $), then way B, the middle-half band across the six rows, as the tranche band, and then the step-by-step: seven numbered steps from the peer set to the price range, every number checkable by eye. Under it, COMPARE puts two or three companies on one axis where today is 0% for each, so upside is upside across companies. Outliers and blanks are named on every row and the band is stated with and without them. The same arithmetic runs in a node script and in the browser, and the tests feed a snapshot in and check the same numbers come out.

Where each number comes from

  • Who the peers are: ticker_cohorts. The company's cohort is chosen as the round-3 pages chose it (the coordinator's defaults for MU, NVDA, GEV, VST; otherwise the first tag that is not a size tag). The other tags are named in step 1. Funds in the cohort are excluded by name.
  • The multiples: the same code the live comps pages run (deliverables/20260927/comps-r3/r3.mjs → compsRead) on the same tables: fundamentals (EPS TTM, revenue TTM, market value and the price on that row → shares), analyst_estimates (this year's and next year's EPS → forward P/E, growth, PEG), fundamentals_history (revenue and EBITDA by quarter, four added up), balance_history (net debt at the newest balance → enterprise value), company_profile (names, fund or not). All of it is FMP data as the Hub stores it. P/S is computed on the same inputs, as on the 28 Sep page.
  • Today's price: the chart API /quotes for every name; for the company on screen the Hub's own live quote when it has one (the stamp says which). If the quotes cannot be reached, the fundamentals row's dated price stands in and the stamp says so in red.
  • The percentiles: peerBand, straight-line between the two nearest peers, on the peers left in a row (positive multiples under the caps: P/E 100x, EV/sales and P/S 50x, EV/EBITDA 100x, PEG 10x).
  • The implied prices: P/E rows multiple × EPS; EV rows (multiple × revenue or EBITDA − net debt) ÷ shares; P/S multiple × revenue ÷ shares; PEG × growth % × EPS estimate. A negative implied value shows as zero, as on the live page.
  • Way B: middleHalfBand from the approved deliverables/20260928/comps-r3-labels/labels-r3.mjs, unchanged. Ways A and C stay off the page, as decided.
  • Outliers: Tukey's rule per row (beyond 1.5 × the middle half); with fewer than four peers nobody is called one. The "without" band reprices the row on the remaining peers with the same arithmetic.
  • The code: deliverables/20260929/comps-live/ladder.mjs (the seven steps, the outlier readings, the compare set — pure), snapshot-live.mjs (the one snapshot-taker used by the browser and by snapshot.mjs on this Mac), draw.mjs (the drawing, ported from the 28 Sep page with every class prefixed), live.mjs (the mount), and in index.html the section estCompsHTML and the hook compsLiveMount. Tests: tests/comps-live.test.mjs (14). Proof script: shots.mjs.

What could be wrong

  • MU's forward P/E is 6.8x because the "current fiscal year" estimate on file is FY ending Aug 2027, EPS $158.42, three and a half times the trailing $44.18. That makes the forward row imply $5,398 at the median and stretches the envelope to $8,749. Way B is built so one such row cannot drag the band, and the ladder shows the figure and its date so it can be questioned; but whether that estimate row is the right "current year" is a data question for the estimates feed, not for this page.
  • TSM at 1.1x on the P/E rows is the known unit fault (EPS stored in a different unit from the price). It stays in, named as the LOWEST PEER; with fewer outliers than four peers on a row it cannot be called one. The board already prints "in TWD" for it.
  • Thin cohorts. GEV's rows rest on 2 to 5 peers; a median of two is the average of two names. The rows say the count, and the sentence for GEV should be read that way.
  • The pane is narrow. On the Hub the ESTIMATES pane is about 450 px wide even on a 1680 screen, so the ladder sits under the field, not beside it (it moves beside only when the box is 1180 px or wider, which the pane never is today; EXPAND may change that). The wide tables of steps 2 and 3 scroll sideways.
  • The snapshot is ten minutes old at most inside one session; the price on the stamp is the Hub's live quote, the peers' prices are the chart API's at the time of the read.
  • The "comp" gauge at index.html ~17564 that the brief points at is the Geiger COMPOSITE (data-gs="comp"), not the comps; it was not touched.
  • Font sizes inside the section are 11 px and up; the rest of the ESTIMATES tab uses the Hub's smaller sizes, so the section looks a little larger than its neighbours.

What was not done

  • Not deployed, not pushed. The branch is hub/comps-live-20260929 from live 9330845.
  • No new tab: the section lives in ESTIMATES (the tab bar is nine tabs on keys 1–9). A COMPS tab of its own is a decision for Alan.
  • The cohort a company runs on is not switchable on the page yet (step 1 names the other tags). The 27 Sep comps page has a switch; adding it here is a small step.
  • No change to the 27/28 Sep deliverable pages, to prototypes/indicator-lab/, lab.html or INDICATOR_LAB/.
  • No visible browser was ever opened; every screen is headless and the browser was closed after each run.

Decisions for Alan at most three, with a recommendation

  1. Where the comps live: inside ESTIMATES under the analysts' target (as built), or a COMPS tab of its own. Recommendation: keep it in ESTIMATES for now — the target and the comps read best side by side, and the tab bar stays at nine.
  2. The compare axis: percent from today (as built: upside is upside), or dollars. Recommendation: percent; a dollar axis cannot hold a $228 name and a $1,072 name on one line.
  3. The default reading when outliers change the band: the band with them in is the headline (as built), the band without them right under it. Recommendation: keep "in" as the headline until the table is agreed, as on 28 Sep; the two are always both on the page.
