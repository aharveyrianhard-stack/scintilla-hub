4 · The Station tape

This is one 34 px strip, like the iOS Stocks widget. First come the page's six names, each with a 30-day sparkline, last price and day change, coloured by the day. Then come earnings in the next N days, which you can set to 7, 14 or 21. Then the latest scintillas. It swipes left to right as you described, with a switch to reverse it, and pauses under the mouse. It hides itself 5 s after the mouse leaves the bottom edge. It comes back when the mouse comes near the bottom or a new scintilla lands. The names are the deck's current page, six a page, as the Station's cohort pages show them. Prices refresh once a minute, the tape every two minutes and sparklines every five.

What it costs: while it shows and swipes, the strip adds about 2 CPU-seconds a minute above an empty tab. That is the one continuous animation. Once hidden it pauses and costs nothing measurable. For scale, the live Hub dashboard measured 9.2 CPU-seconds a minute on the same machine.

What the costs say: the three Geiger-in-motion players are the expensive ones, 6.9 to 11.3 CPU-seconds a minute while they play. That is as much as or more than the whole live dashboard (9.2). Build A for the dashboard, with the expanded view from section 3. A moves the least. The board stays where your eye expects it, every column stays, and the fix is mostly taking things away: the company bar, the second CLOSED, the second timeframe row, the extra META. B's main idea, the chart always on screen with the tabs, is what you asked for in the expanded view, and the expanded view is the same in A and B. So A gets that idea where there is room for it. One more cost to know about. The widening Geiger cell's eight-timeframe strip needs each name's per-timeframe numbers. Today that means the chart API's detail read. For 72 names that is about 420 KB in three calls, which is fine once a minute but heavy for a phone. A small chart API change, adding the eight timeframe composites to the plain Geiger read, would remove it.

Decisions for you

  1. A or B for the dashboard? I recommend A, with the expanded view from section 3.
  2. The two price tapes at the bottom, MACRO and ALL. They carry prices, not events, so they are not in the one tape. I recommend keeping them as one price lane at the bottom, hidden until the mouse comes near, as they already are. The other choice is to drop them.
  3. Cyan. The live Hub uses cyan for its chrome, meaning active tabs, lines and labels. The 23 Sep rule says monochrome except direction. These mockups follow the rule. I recommend monochrome with the amber status, and Hub 1 would remove the cyan everywhere.

What could be wrong

  • It is Sunday. The scintillas strip was empty, prices were Friday's, and nothing moved. The tape's scintillas are Friday's, and a busy weekday tape is longer.
  • The mockup chart is a plain line from /candles, not the Station chart with clouds and RSI. The build keeps the Station pane.
  • Crypto has no price in the mockups. The chart API does not stream it; the Hub gets it from Coinbase directly.
  • The RSI column in the mockups is the Geiger's daily RSI. The Hub's RSI column is FMP's. They can differ by a point or two.
  • In the Fundamentals screenshot the $1.43T market cap comes from the stored fundamentals row, which was priced on 20 Sep. The board's $1.9T comes from the company profile. I did not change either.
  • Markers inside the chart iframe and inside the Geiger and Fundamentals panels are placed from the screenshots, not measured, because those parts are drawn by another page. Each such marker says so on hover.
  • The CPU runs overlapped my own screenshot runs for a few minutes. Layout A measured 0.93 and then 0.41 on two runs, so small differences are noise. The motion players' 7 to 11 are not.
  • The Hub's test suite fails five tests on the base commit before this lane, not the two the common brief names. This lane adds nine tests, all passing, and no new failures.

What I did not do

  • No change to the Hub's index.html, the Station, the database or the chart API. No deploy, no push, no schedule.
  • COHORT COMPARE, ROTATION, RELATIVE and the tabs FINANCIALS, NEWS, SOCIAL, EVENTS and READ are not redesigned. The mockups say so where they would be.
  • The rewind player is not in the mockups. It keeps its place under the cohort Geiger.
  • The Station tape is not placed on a real Station page; it runs on a stand-in page.
