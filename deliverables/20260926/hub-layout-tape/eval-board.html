Visuals evaluation board

Every visual proposal made so far, on one page. Each card has a picture of it running, what it is for, where it would live, and what it costs your machine to keep it on screen. Under each card, pick keep, change or drop and write one line. When you are done, press copy my scores and paste the text to the coordinator: that text is what the revamp gets decided from.
Your picks are kept in this browser only, until you copy them. Nobody else sees them, and nothing is sent anywhere. Cost is measured the same way for every card: the page opened headless on this MacBook at 1680 × 1050, the CPU used in its first 45 seconds (load), then the CPU it keeps using per minute once settled (steady), for the whole browser.
