4 · the method in plain words — the characters

The heat map
Eleven sectors in one strip, hottest left. Each column reads four things live: our names (the Hub's Geiger averaged over the sector's served companies), funds (the mean of three cap-weight sector funds — State Street, iShares, Vanguard — "more than just the State Street"), the bow tie (the equal-weight fund minus that cap-weight mean: below zero the giants carry the sector) and rot 72 (the sector fund's 72-session return over SPY). The column's heat is its average rank on names, funds and rotation; the top three are HOT, the bottom three COLD. B1's market-wide read (every computed name at the 2026-10-02 close) sits under each column with its SOUND / THIN verdict and is shown, never used where THIN.
The themes
Inside each hot sector, the Hub's cohorts (tabs, registry, T12's candidates) with T12's soundness: eight or more names read, standard error at or under 0.10, held on each of the six closes, one industry at 60% or more. A thin theme is listed grey with why; a MIXED one (under half its companies in one sector, like BLUE CHIP or THEMATIC) is listed, never run. The three soundest run. When a sector has no sound theme today (Energy, Discretionary, Staples), its own served names run as one ring, marked FALLBACK.
The knockout ring
Every member of a theme steps in with four readings. Comps: C5's business-first peer set, the field with the outliers out, the way-C centre (every peer's implied price on every priced measure, weighted, the median of them all) — the reading is the upside from today's price. Target: the firms' newest cleaned note inside 183 days — the median's upside and how many firms. Revisions: raised minus lowered in the last 30 days. Geiger: the member's live Geiger against the ring's average ("oversold, but less than the others"). Each reading becomes a rank inside the ring (0 worst … 1 best) — no universal threshold anywhere — and the score is the weighted mean of the ranks the member has (comps 50 · target 20 · revisions 10 · Geiger 20; a blank is a named blank, not a zero). The simple way to kick off outliers: a member whose comps upside sits more than 3 MAD from the ring's median upside is a CANDIDATE FOR ELIMINATION — hollow, with its value, out of the ranking (today: MU +316%, F +1,287%, PDD, GM, QCOM, SIMO …). The top three survive.
The picks
The survivors of the hot sectors' rings, one card each, with the reasons in one line and the levels the operator still decides: today's price, the way-C low · centre · high, the target low · median · high. No line is drawn.
The discussion
Every name that stepped into a ring today (hot and cold sectors) scored by the full chain — the sector's heat rank (weight 1) and its knockout score (weight 2) — and the five strongest and five weakest named with their reasons. The discussion starts from the model, not from a blank.
The dials
Hot/cold sectors 3 · cohorts per sector 3 · heat weights 1/1/1 (bow tie 0) · knockout weights 50/20/10/20 · outlier fence 3 MAD · survivors 3 · chain weights 1/2. The page shows the answer under the baselines and re-runs when a dial moves; RESET brings the baselines back.

6 · where each number comes from, and what could be wrong

Geiger: the Hub's live reading (/geiger) for the 590 served symbols and the 44 sector funds, 2026-10-05T15:49:32.495Z. Bars: the chart API's daily bars to 2026-10-02 (today's session is not a finished daily bar, so rotation is as of Friday's close). Comps: C5's method and code on this branch's files (segments pulled on Fly 3 Oct), one table read per ring over the union of the members' sets. Targets: analyst_target_news with A4's quality columns, 3191 clean rows for 169 names. Cohorts and soundness: T12's measured file of 3 Oct. B1: the 2 Oct close. The data file took 11 s, 233 chart API calls and 165 table reads; it is dated and the page prints every age.

What could be wrong: the comps upside depends on the business-first set (a set under two peers is a blank); many upsides are large because the field's centre sits far from the price for whole industries (semiconductors today) — the fence handles the extremes, the rank handles the rest, and the operator reads the centre, not the percentage. A target with few firms is a blank under three. A sector's rotation is its fund's, a cohort's is its median member's. The fallback rings (sector's own names) have no six-close test. The heat moves during the session with the live Geiger; T12 and B1 are dated. Nothing here sizes a position or sets cash: the July MODEL sheet's heat → condition → % invested stays step 1 and was not re-invented.

What was not done: the ALLOCATION master tab still points at /allocation/ — index.html is untouched on this branch (nothing changes on the Hub without Alan's approval). No table was written. The page reads a dated data file built from this Mac; a scheduled rebuild (a Fly job or an edge function) is not set up.
