What this page shows. Every tradeable instrument the Hub serves (companies and funds), one Hub cell each: the ticker, today's Geiger reading and the Hub's own Geiger track (green from the centre for a positive reading, red for a negative one; an empty dashed track = no reading yet). The cells sit in sector blocks that tile the whole canvas; inside a block they run green → red. Every cell is the same size and every ticker prints at the first view — nothing is drawn on top of anything else.

Zoom = level. Scroll to zoom toward the pointer, drag to pan, double-click the background or press RESET for the whole market. Past 1.5 × the cells regroup under their industry (FMP's industry; funds under FUNDS); past 2.5 × they split by cohort (the registry's home cohort; a cohort's caption opens its podium); past 4 × each cell shows its second line — the name, the day's move and the market cap. The type follows one rule (label-scale.mjs): 12 px at home, never under 11 px, never over 24 px on a 1080-tall screen; zoomed out, a ticker that would overlap its neighbour's thins out (strongest reading first) and comes back as you zoom in.

The podium. PODIUM on a block's title, a cohort's caption, a MY LISTS tab or the card opens the set as the 3D bow tie: V3's laser coil — the green half rising from the zero ring, first place at the centre and the top, fanned down and outwards to zero; the red half the mirror below it, down to the deepest red at the centre. The translucent sphere around the set is its total market cap ($1 T = 80 units of radius; a fund with no cap on file is listed, not counted), so two sets rank by size at a glance. Drag to turn any way, scroll to zoom, double-click or RESET VIEW = the 70° view. WALK (or ↓) walks the steps from the first place down the green half, across the ring and down the red half: ↓ ↑ J K the wheel, Home / End, Esc leaves. Click a step for its card.

Where each number comes from. The Geiger readings are read live from the chart API (/geiger for every Hub line; the nightly scout Geiger for the off-Hub names, drawn the same). The tree is tree.json (the r3 market tree with the 28 Sep cohort level); industries are industry-20261002.json (FMP, the authority); market caps are data/market-caps-20261005.json (company_profile.market_cap through the Hub's public read path, with tree.json's market value as the fallback for a company). MY LISTS read the Hub's mirror in this browser (sc_lists, sc_fav); the Hub's server copy is the source of truth.

The parts. Bar = the Hub's GEIGER tile track (gs-cgr); type, colours, the tab rows (ctabs) and the table (mytab) are the Hub's own CSS, copied by tools/extract-hub-parts.mjs into hub-parts.css — never redrawn here. 3D = V3's coil3d.js, copied whole (plus the walk and the click).

T14 · one connected market (5 Oct, night). The first band is the market's indexes: the broad five (SPY QQQ DIA IWM RSP) and the eleven sector SPDRs, one Hub cell each, cyan tickers. A line runs from each index to every sector it holds, as thick as the weight (the label is the weight: SPY → TECHNOLOGY 39 %), quiet until you hover the index or the sector. The weight is the share of the WHOLE fund; what the Hub cannot place by sector is "unclassified" and printed on the index's card (IWM: 89 % of its 1,988 small caps are off the Hub's sector map, so its lines are thin and honest).

A ticker's connections. Click a name: lines run from its cell to every place it lives — its sector (dashed), its home cohort, every index and fund that holds it (cyan, the label is the weight), its comps peers in other sectors (the second ink). The card lists the same with the weights, every cohort it belongs to, and each peer's sector and whether it is on the Hub live (●) or at the close tier (○). Suppliers and customers: no data exists on the Hub — no table holds them and the FMP supply-chain route is not loaded — so the card says so instead of guessing.

Cohorts across sectors. Two cohorts in different sectors are linked when they share members, comps peers or a narrow fund (a sector or industry fund — the broad and style funds hold everything and would link everything). One line per link, its count in the label (members + peer pairs + funds), between the cohorts' captions at the COHORT level and between the middle of their cells before that. LINKS shows the top 40 by count, all 310, or none; hover a cohort's caption or any of its cells to light its links. Example: AI HARDWARE (Technology) ↔ AI POWER (Utilities): 9 = ETN in both, 5 peer pairs, 3 funds (AGIX, PAVE, XLI).

The outline. The left column is the same tree as a list — Market → Indexes → Sectors → Cohorts → Names — and it follows the canvas: the row you are on is marked cyan, its branch opens, and clicking a row moves the canvas there. Every name carries its mark: ● on the Hub live (a Geiger bar on the chart API), ○ at the close tier (the nightly scout Geiger), · no reading. OUTLINE ◧ hides the column to give the canvas the width back.

Where these come from. data/connections-20261005.json, built by build-connections.mjs from four dated read-only exports (data/raw-20261005/: etf_holdings — 62 funds, 12,795 rows; company_profile sectors; peer_sources — FMP ∪ Massive, loaded 1 Oct; ticker_cohorts) and tree.json. Nothing is written anywhere.

What could be wrong. A reading missing on the chart API prints as an empty track. Cells are sized to fit the screen the page opened on; a resize re-lays them out. The black share of the canvas is the Hub's black between the Hub's parts, not a layout gap.
