The price source files history by ticker letters. SERV was ServiceMaster until 2020 and has been Serve Robotics since April 2024, so asking for "SERV since 2003" returns both companies in one line. Every long average, ladder rung and Geiger read over those years mixes them.

How the trim works. The tool asks the price provider for each company's own first day. That's the later of its listing date and the date it took the ticker. It checks that date against the silence in the stored prices, and trims only when the two agree. It saves a copy of every file before changing it. It also records the first day, so a future rebuild can never pull the old company's prices back in.

The 11 RSP funds are the opposite case: their earlier years are stored under their old tickers (RYT and the others). Nothing wrong is mixed in; they just have fewer years. Joining the old ticker's years on would be a separate job.
