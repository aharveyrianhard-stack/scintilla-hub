8 · What could be wrong

  • Keyword counts are only as good as the keywords. "Apple" also counts Apple Watch videos; "Arm" is too short to count, so ARM is counted by $ARM only. The rule is printed on the tab, but it is a rule I chose (decision 1 below).
  • The X feed was 46 hours old when I shot this, so "last 24 h" reads 0 on X for everything. That is the collector's freshness, not this tab's; the tab prints the feed's newest post time.
  • The RSI fan's colours are the Station's: in the Hub's narrow pane the six lines are all shades of blue, so they are hard to tell apart. The legend gives each value. One line (8H) carries "18 AUG" in its label, meaning the Station has no newer 8-hour data. I did not change the Station.
  • Fiscal quarters are the company's own (MU's Q3 FY26 ended May 2026), not calendar quarters. The table's footnote says so.

9 · What I did not do

  • ESTIMATES' own inside layout is unchanged: small chart labels (under 11 px) and the cyan QUARTERLY/ANNUAL button are from earlier rounds. It has more room, not a redesign.
  • The EVENTS tab was not matched to the events revamp; that needs the revamp's branch.
  • No separate "ladder view": the ladder is the full width of the GEIGER tab, 420 px tall.
  • STATS hides the market cap's "as of" date line in its narrow column. FUNDAMENTALS still prints the FMP update date.
  • The trend-strong / momentum-peeling signal (ARM) is for Statistics (section E); nothing was built for it here.
  • No push, no deploy, no database write, no paid API, no Station change. Test browsers were headless and closed after each run.
