The company view, round 2

You said the company view was hard to get around, that you liked the moving-average thing, and that the RSI should simply be a pane on a Station chart. This is that view, built into the Hub on a branch and working on live data. The Station chart, with its clouds and one RSI pane, is always on top. Under it sit six key numbers and the tabs. The moving-average ladder is redrawn so all nine averages are readable. EXPAND gives you a list of names down the left side so you can move between companies without going back to the board.

STATUS: built and checked headless on this MacBook. Nothing pushed, nothing deployed. The coordinator deploys the Station first, then the Hub.

3 · The six key numbers — what each one reads

All six come from data the Hub already loads when you open a company. None of them needs a new request.

TileWhat it showsWhere it comes fromWhat could be wrong
GEIGERThe composite, −1 to +1, with its small bar and "bull lean", "bear lean" or "neutral" (neutral within ±0.10).The chart API's provider Geiger: the same number as the board's GEIGER column.For a name the provider does not cover, it shows a dash.
TODAYToday's change, and how many of its usual days that is ("0.1× its usual ±4.9%").The change comes from Massive's live quote (the board's). The usual day comes from ticker_heartbeat_daily.usual_day_60 (its typical daily move over 60 sessions).A name with no usual-day row in the last three weeks shows only the change.
MKT CAPToday's market cap.company_profile.market_cap (FMP's profile, refreshed daily), the same number as the board. It is never fundamentals.market_cap, which is the value at the last quarter end, and in the filing currency for foreign filers.If the profile has no value, the tile falls back to the board row's number, which comes from the same table.
FWD P/EThe price divided by the next twelve months' EPS estimate ("NTM").analyst_estimates, using exactly the ESTIMATES tab's own calculation (fwdTrailPE).If the estimates are in a foreign currency, the tile shows a dash rather than a wrong multiple. A fund shows "a fund".
NEXT REPORTThe next earnings date, how many days away it is, and whether it is before the open or after the close.earnings_events: the first date on or after today.If no future date is on file, it says "none on file".
TARGET MEDIANThe analysts' median price target, how far it is above or below the price, and how many analysts.price_target_consensus.target_median.A median can be stale if analysts have not updated it. A fund shows "a fund".

While the company's data is still arriving (about one to four seconds), the four tiles that need it show "…". They fill in place and the chart is not touched.

6 · What changed on the Station

  • S1 merged onto today's Station. This includes the Hub pane mode (?bare=hub: no doubled toolbar, its own price updates, and no 5.5-second wait), the "last bar" time stamp that turns amber when the line stops, and the rule that a same-name page change keeps the line. The merge had no conflicts. I checked that the Geiger bar, ES/NQ, admission v2 and rotation are all still in the three chart files. The test suite has the same 18 known failures before and after (751 tests).
  • One RSI of the chart's own timeframe. The chart now accepts ?rsi=chart, and ?rsi=1h, 4h, 1D, 3D and 1W, and draws a single solid line. The Lab's six-line fan (?rsi=1) is unchanged. The Hub sends the chart's own timeframe. A Station without this change still draws 4h and 1D, and shows no RSI for 1h, 3D and 1W (never a wrong one). That is why the Station goes first.
  • Market cap is today's everywhere. Analytics, the fundamentals template (comps, peer ranking and the WACC weight) and the DCF template now read company_profile.market_cap first. So does the Hub's copy of the fundamentals page. fundamentals.market_cap is used only when the profile has no value.

7 · What could be wrong, and what I did not do

  • The collapsed view is tight. Beside the board at 1680, the company panel is about 640 px wide. The chart there is only about 220 px tall, with the RSI inside it. EXPAND is where the chart is big. See decision 1.
  • The name, price and usual day now appear twice when a company is open. They are in the Hub's full-width bar at the top and on the company line. I left the top bar alone because other rooms use it. See decision 2.
  • The per-timeframe table depends on one chart API call (/geiger?detail=1, 5-second limit). When it does not answer, the table says so in words instead of showing numbers. This happened in some of my runs when the API was slow.
  • Before the Station is deployed, the Hub's 1h, 3D and 1W charts show no RSI pane.
  • Not done: no push, no deploy, no database change. The Indicator Lab is byte-identical. No other Hub room was changed, except that the global board repaint now also refreshes the rail (only when EXPAND is on). The funds' FINANCIALS tab keeps its label (the old view renamed it HOLDINGS in one place). I did not remove the old functions for the CHART tab and the legacy header; nothing reaches them now.
  • How the pictures were taken: Playwright's Chromium, headless (never on your screen), and killed after each run. For "after", requests to scintillahub.ai and station.scintillahub.ai were answered from the two branch folders, so the chart API saw its real origins and nothing had to be relaxed. Every write (Supabase or /api POST) was answered locally and recorded instead of being sent. None happened.

8 · Decisions for you

  1. Give the company panel more room when a company is open, without EXPAND? Today the board keeps about 60% of the width. Recommendation: keep it as is for now and use EXPAND (Esc brings you back). If you find yourself always expanding, make expanded the default. That is one stored setting.
  2. Hide the Hub's full-width name bar while a company is open? The company line repeats its name, price and usual day. Recommendation: yes, but in a separate small change after you have seen this one, because the bar also carries the ECONOMIC nudge.
  3. RSI colour. The single line uses the Station's RSI indigo, with the 70 line in green and the 30 line in red, exactly as the Station draws it. Recommendation: keep it, since it is the Station's own chart.
