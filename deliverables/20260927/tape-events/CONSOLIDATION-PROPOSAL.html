ONE MARKET AREA: A PROPOSAL

27 Sep 2026 · design only, nothing built (your note: "too many tabs and sub-tabs… same coin")

The problem, counted

Comparing groups of stocks is spread across 4 tabs (COHORT COMPARE · MAP · ROTATION · RELATIVE), 2 modes inside COHORT COMPARE (COHORTS · SECTORS), and 5 families under SECTORS (MEMBERS · SPDR · iSHARES · VANGUARD · EQUAL-WT). The same four tabs appear twice: in the dashboard's right pane and full-screen in SCENES. The timeframe chips (60M · 4H · 1D · 1W) drive MAP, ROTATION and RELATIVE, but not COHORT COMPARE. That one has its own scale, so switching tabs can silently change the time you are looking at.

the chosen lens, drawn for the chosen set at the chosen timeframe
  • WHAT is the set of groups: cohorts, or sectors in one fund family.
  • WHEN is one clock for everything. It is the MAP's selector, as you asked.
  • HOW is the picture: STRENGTH (today's COHORT COMPARE bars), MAP (tiles), ROTATION (the quadrant chart), RELATIVE (the lines).

Changing HOW never changes WHAT or WHEN, and changing WHEN changes every picture together. That is the "same coin". Every chip row is one line, and there is never a chip row inside a chip row.

What has to be true first

  • STRENGTH at a timeframe. Cohort compare averages the Geiger, which today is one daily number. The store has a Geiger per timeframe (composite_staged carries a tf), so this can follow WHEN. It needs checking that 60M and 4H rows are fresh before it is built.
  • Sectors in ROTATION and RELATIVE. Both need price series for the fund families. The SPDR set exists; the other three families need checking on the chart API.

Your decisions

  1. Name of the area. Recommended: MARKET. It replaces the four tabs in the right pane, and SCENES becomes its full-screen view.
  2. Should STRENGTH follow the timeframe? Recommended: yes, once the 60M / 4H Geiger rows are confirmed fresh. Until then it would say "daily only" beside the chip.
  3. Default lens. Recommended: MAP, which is what the pane opens on today.
