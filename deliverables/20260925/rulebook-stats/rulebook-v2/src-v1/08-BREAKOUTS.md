## BLOCK ONE — THE CHAIN

---

**B-1 — THE BREAK CHAIN (ONE GRAMMAR, FIVE REFERENCES)**

- RULE: The **break chain** is the named composition of existing law: a reference (below) → C-1 claim through its zone → exactly one of C-2 CONFIRMED / C-3 VOID / C-6 EXPIRED → on confirmation, the C-2 verdict (finite `cfm.verdictClock`) → aftermath per Chapter 9. The **five admissible reference types**, each already exposing the L-12 tuple: **HORIZONTAL LEVEL** (CH 4) · **DIAGONAL** (D-4) · **CHANNEL BOUNDARY** (D-5/D-7) · **RANGE BOUNDARY** (R-6) · **STRUCTURE FLOOR/CEILING** (S-7). No sixth type exists until a chapter defines one and this card is amended. Every chain event stamps its reference type and inherits that type's evidence record (episodes, classes, maturity, walk state).
- CONFIRMATION: Each stage's own law confirms it; this card adds no new event.
- INVALIDATION: A "breakout" cited against anything not on the five-type list — a round number with no level ledger, a hand-drawn line, an indicator value — is malformed (D-9, V-19 discipline).
- WHY IT WORKS: The field writes five breakout chapters and lets them disagree. One chain over five references is why a range break, a trendline break, and a structure break can be compared, counted, and tested as the same species of event — which they are.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-2 — ATTEMPT TAXONOMY & THE RESET RULE**

- RULE: Over C-11's per-direction counters, the chain's vocabulary: **ATTEMPT** = claim opened · **FAILED ATTEMPT** = terminal without confirmation, subtyped **TRAPPED-FAIL** (C-3 void — entrants were closed against) and **LAPSED-FAIL** (C-6 expiry — the attack died of boredom) · **BREAK** = C-2 confirmed. **The reset rule (this chapter's ownership, deposited since C-11):** both directions' counters on a reference reset to zero on (a) a CONFIRMED break in either direction, or (b) the reference's retirement (L-10). Nothing else resets them — not role flips, not defense, not time. Attempt counts are evidence feeding L-7's odds table and B-4's grade; they gate nothing.
- CONFIRMATION: Counter transitions at each terminal event's t_close.
- INVALIDATION: A count surviving a confirmed break, or reset by anything off the list, corrupts the ledger and every odds read from it.
- WHY IT WORKS: "Third attempt" only predicts if attempts are counted one way and wiped when the question they were counting toward — will it break? — gets answered. A confirmed break answers it; the ledger starts a new question.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-3 — PRE-BREAK PRESSURE**

- RULE: A claim carries the **PRESSURE** annotation when, within `brk.pressureClock` bars before the claim bar, any of the following stand at the reference: an E-7 compression event whose bars sit zone-adjacent (R-8's COILED at range boundaries; the same test generalized to all five types); an inside-bar chain (E-5) zone-adjacent; or **tightening tests** — the last `brk.tightCount` episode reactions at the reference monotonically decreasing in size (read from L-6's reaction records). PRESSURE is a grade input (B-4), never a requirement and never a verdict.
- CONFIRMATION: The constituent E/L facts at their own t_closes; the annotation joins the claim when it opens.
- INVALIDATION: PRESSURE read from compression inside a range's NMZ (R-3), or from candidate-swing reactions, is void.
- WHY IT WORKS: Shrinking reactions at a defended price mean the defenders are absorbing less each visit — the spring compressing. Every classical author gestures at this; the episode ledger lets it be counted instead of felt.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-4 — THE GRADE JOIN (WHICH EVIDENCE IS LEGIBLE TO WHICH BREAK)**

- RULE: The C-10 grade of a confirmed break is computed from the grader list joined by reference type — the join table this card fixes:
  - **All five types:** penetration distance · trend-bar close (E-2) · PRESSURE (B-3) · opposite-direction reject within `cfm.gradeLookback` (E-4) · breakaway gap class on the claim bar (E-12).
  - **Horizontal level adds:** WATCH state (L-7) · cluster membership MULTI(k) (L-11/B-9).
  - **Range boundary adds:** maturity (R-5) · the boundary's defense-class record (R-4).
  - **Channel boundary adds:** WALK state at exit (D-7) — with-walk exits and against-walk exits grade *differently* (against-walk is Chapter 9's trap material).
  - **Structure floor/ceiling adds:** DEEP-PULLBACK WATCH (S-9) · the trend's PAUSED state (S-10).
  **Volume row (CH 10 cashed, joins all five types):** Q-FUND (relVol funding of the claim/confirming bar) · Q-EVR (effort-vs-result class of the confirming bar) — per Q-3/Q-7; gap-borne breaks additionally read the claim bar's CLIMAX state (Q-6, via B-10). Grades order evidence; C-9's wall stands — nothing here creates, rescues, or vetoes a confirmation.
- CONFIRMATION: Grade computed at the C-2 event from stamped facts only.
- INVALIDATION: A grader read across reference types without a row in this table, or a grade gating verdict existence, fails at review (C-10).
- WHY IT WORKS: Evidence is type-specific — range maturity means nothing to a trendline; walk state means nothing to a level. The join table is where the book says which witness may testify at which trial.
- STATUS: DRAFT

## BLOCK TWO — ENTRIES AND OBJECTIVES

---

**B-5 — ENTRY DOCTRINES (ON-CONFIRM vs ON-RETEST)**

- RULE: Exactly two entry events exist on the chain: **ENTRY-C** = the C-2 confirming close itself; **ENTRY-R** = the retest HOLD event (V-24's exact event; the machinery around it is Chapter 9's). Their costs, stated as law and measured in the ledger: ENTRY-C pays the worse price, is present for every runaway (B-7), and eats every confirmed-then-failed break; ENTRY-R pays the better price, misses every no-retest break, and avoids every failure that happens before the retest holds. The honesty table `brk.entryOdds` publishes, per reference type × grade: fill frequency, throwback frequency (Bulkowski's tables put retrace-to-break near 60% for most patterns — **the retest is the norm, not the exception**), runaway frequency, and failure timing. **The doctrine choice is not this book's to make:** it is a CH 16 legal knob (profile-keyed), and the invariance law holds — both doctrines read the same events.
- CONFIRMATION: Each entry event is its underlying law's event.
- INVALIDATION: Any third entry event ("early entry on the claim," intrabar stop-runs) is malformed — claims are not confirmations (C-1), and intrabar is rumor (V-8).
- WHY IT WORKS: The oldest argument at every prop desk is confirm-versus-retest, and it has never been resolvable in prose because it is a *price-of-certainty* trade-off that varies by context. So the book prices it, per context, in a table — and hands the choice to the reader's risk profile, where it always belonged.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-6 — MEASURED OBJECTIVES (THE SINGLE GRAMMAR)**

- RULE: One projection grammar for the whole book (patterns will parameterize it, never redefine it — assessment §5 honored). Method options (`brk.objMethod`), projection base per `brk.objBase` ∈ {BREAK-LEVEL, CONFIRM-CLOSE}:
  - **OBJ-H (height):** project the reference's own height — range height (R), channel width (D-5), pattern height when CH 12 instantiates, structure leg (S-8's impulse) for BOS.
  - **OBJ-SW (swing projection):** project the pre-break impulse leg (AB=CD) from the base.
  - **OBJ-ATR:** `brk.objAtrMult × ATR` at the confirming bar.
  - **OBJ-NEXT:** the nearest ACTIVE reference zone beyond the break in the break's direction, highest rank first (`ref.rankOrder`) — the "next level on record."
  - **OBJ-MG (measuring gap):** where an E-13 continuation gap prints inside the post-break move, project the pre-gap distance beyond the gap — the classical folklore, admitted strictly as a testable option (E-13's deposit cashed).
  A set objective becomes a reference *price* (taggable, V-9) — never a verdict. Its tag fires **OBJECTIVE-MET (PAID)**, consumed by Chapter 9 (post-objective law) and CH 16 (scale-out knob). Every option publishes with its hit-rate honesty column `brk.objOdds` per reference type; unpublished projections may not appear in analysis (B-11).
- CONFIRMATION: Objective computable at the C-2 event; PAID at the tag.
- INVALIDATION: A projection mixing options, re-based mid-flight, or extended after PAID without a fresh reference (Chapter 9's anti-greed law) is void.
- WHY IT WORKS: Every projection method is a folk theorem with a real hit rate. The grammar makes them one machine with five settings; the ledger makes each setting confess its number before it is allowed in print.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-7 — THE RUNAWAY BREAK**

- RULE: A confirmed break stamps **RUNAWAY** when `retest.clock` expires with no retest (V-24/A-1's NO-RETEST) **and** the close at that expiry is ≥ `brk.runawayDist` (scale units) beyond the zone. RUNAWAY is evidence: a one-sided auction witness for CH 13's regime reading (deposit) and the trigger for CH 16's chase-discipline knob — the book's entire answer to "it left without me" is the measured cost of each response, not a chase rule. Runaway frequency lives in `brk.entryOdds` (it is ENTRY-R's miss rate).
- CONFIRMATION: The clock expiry + distance test at t_close.
- INVALIDATION: RUNAWAY read before the clock expires, or with the close back within `brk.runawayDist`, is premature and void.
- WHY IT WORKS: A break that never looks back is the market with no sellers left to test it — the strongest and least enterable of breaks. Naming it keeps its absence honest too: most breaks *do* look back, which is ENTRY-R's whole case.
- STATUS: DRAFT

## BLOCK THREE — COMPOSITE CASES AND THE LEDGER

---

**B-8 — FAILURE ROUTING (THE HANDOFF TO NINE)**

- RULE: Every non-clean outcome routes, and this chapter keeps nothing: **C-3 void** → Chapter 9's fakeout setup (A-3), with reference-type context attached (LOADED-OPPOSITE at range boundaries, against-walk at channels, failed structural attack at floors). **Confirmed, then retest FAILS** → Chapter 9's verdict-reset machinery (A-2): the break verdict dies there, the reference reverts per L-9. **C-6 expiry** → counter increments (B-2), nothing else — expiry is boredom, not information about direction. This card creates no events; it is the routing law (S-11's pattern).
- CONFIRMATION: Each routed event is stamped by its own law.
- INVALIDATION: Aftermath machinery restated in this chapter fails one-rule-one-home.
- WHY IT WORKS: The chapters stay short because the stumble belongs to exactly one owner. And the reader learns the book's deepest bias early: the failure cases route to the *richer* chapter — because that is where the money is.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-9 — MULTI-REFERENCE BREAKS**

- RULE: One close-beyond may open claims through several references at once (an L-11 cluster; a level and a diagonal crossing; a range boundary that is also the structure floor). Each reference runs **its own chain** (own claims, counters, states — C-11 per reference). The grade join recognizes **MULTI(k)**: a single confirming close through k references stamps MULTI(k) evidence on each (more trapped inventory per B-4). For downstream *gating* (which verdict a consumer reads), the verdict of record is the **highest-ranked reference's** (`ref.rankOrder`, D-8) — one instrument-direction, one governing verdict, no double-counting.
- CONFIRMATION: Deterministic: zone intersections at the confirming close.
- INVALIDATION: Counting one close as k independent confirmations of one thesis (the confluence double-count, L-11's warning) voids the analysis.
- WHY IT WORKS: Breaking three defended prices at once traps three crowds — genuinely stronger. Counting it as three proofs of the same idea is how analysts turn one fact into a conviction. MULTI(k) keeps the strength and refuses the arithmetic.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-10 — GAP-BORNE BREAKS**

- RULE: When the claim bar is a breakaway-gap bar (E-12) that closes beyond the reference zone: the claim is the close (C-8), the gap class is grade evidence (B-4), and the chain runs unchanged. Entry economics differ and are ledgered separately: `brk.gapEntryOdds` — ENTRY-C on a gap-borne confirmation pays the worst price on the chain (the gap already spent much of the move), and ENTRY-R's retest, when it comes, often revisits the gap edge (E-9's fill machinery and the level the surviving edge will birth, L-2). A FAILED-BREAKAWAY finalization (E-14) routes to Chapter 9's gap-trap setup (A-5). The claim bar's CLIMAX state (Q-6) joins `brk.gapEntryOdds` as its volume column (CH 10 cashed).
- CONFIRMATION: The constituent E-8/E-12/C-2 events.
- INVALIDATION: Treating the gap's open as the entry or the claim (intrabar/open-based) is malformed (V-8).
- WHY IT WORKS: Gap breaks are the chain's extreme case — maximum trapped inventory, minimum entry quality. The ledger prices that trade-off instead of letting the drama of the gap make the argument.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**B-11 — THE HONESTY LEDGER (THE PUBLICATION LAW)**

- RULE: This chapter's claims live in published, loop-filled tables: `brk.entryOdds` (B-5) · `brk.objOdds` (B-6) · `brk.gapEntryOdds` (B-10) · joined: `level.testOdds` (L-7), `range.matOdds`/`range.failLoadOdds` (R-5/R-7), gap class × break outcome (E-10…E-14), and — CH 10 cashed — the volume columns: `vol.breakLift` per reference type × grade (Q-7), with the climax column on `brk.gapEntryOdds` (Q-6). **The publication law:** no entry doctrine, objective, or break-quality claim appears in any analysis published under this book's name without its current measured rate attached. An empty cell reads "UNMEASURED" — and an unmeasured claim may be *stated*, never *recommended*. **Regime columns JOINED (CH 13 landed):** every table in this family gains the X-2 state · X-5 climate pair per X-9, and no rate publishes pooled across states (the stated-regime law).
- CONFIRMATION: Editorial: every published rate resolves to a ledger cell with a data vintage.
- INVALIDATION: A naked hit-rate claim ("this usually works") in book prose fails review; a stale vintage (older than the registry's declared refresh) must be flagged.
- WHY IT WORKS: The break chapter is where trading books traditionally lie the most, because it is where the reader most wants to be lied to. The ledger is the chapter's spine: every promise in it arrives with its receipts or with the word UNMEASURED where the receipts should be.
- STATUS: DRAFT
