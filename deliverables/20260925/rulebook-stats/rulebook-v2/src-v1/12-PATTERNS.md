# CHAPTER 12 — PATTERN RULES (P)

**THE SCINTILLA TRADING RULEBOOK · Chapter draft · v1** (supersedes shell v0)
**STATUS: DRAFT** (all cards; promotion by publisher testing) · Sprint 8 ("Finish the Draft") — written with Chapters 15 and 16; the full first draft completes with this trio.

SUMMARY: The cheapest chapter in the book after Diagonals, **by design, and it says so**: every pattern here is a named composition of objects that already exist, triggered by the one break chain that already exists, measured by the one objective grammar that already exists, graded by witnesses already seated, and failed through machinery Chapter 9 already owns. This chapter contributes exactly three things: the compositions (what the names mean, by citation), the candidacy discipline (a pattern is inert until its chain fires), and the honesty tables (Bulkowski's numbers as external seeds, the loop's as promotion). Downstream never needs a pattern's name — regime, aftermath, and risk read the chain, which is why Chapter 13 was written without citing this chapter and lost nothing. If a card below ever seems to need new machinery, that is the card failing, not the machinery.

Compiles against: Chapters 1–11 + 97 §L (X and M are not required: patterns reach them through the chain's stamps, never by name). Cited by: 16 (setup-class participation only).

## BLOCK ONE — THE LAW

---

**P-1 — THE COMPOSITION LAW (PATTERNS DEFINE NO MACHINERY)**

- RULE: A **pattern** is a named composition: a **definition sequence** (cited objects and events from Chapters 1–11 with tolerance predicates in scale units), a **pattern reference** (the level, diagonal, or boundary the composition nominates), and a **trigger** = the ordinary B-1 chain against that reference. From the claim onward the pattern contributes nothing: confirmation is C-2's, failure routes per B-8, aftermath is Chapter 9's, regime treatment rides the chain's stamps (X never learns the pattern's name), objectives use B-6's grammar with pattern-supplied heights (§L), and volume/indicator witnesses grade through the standing joins (Q-3/Q-7, I-9). **Candidacy discipline (V-11's pattern, composition edition):** a detected composition is a **CANDIDATE** — inert: no verdict, no gate, no annotation exported — until the trigger chain's claim opens; candidates die silently by their stated candidate-invalidation or by expiry (`pat.candidateClock`). **The admission rule:** a pattern enters this chapter only if its definition compiles entirely to existing citations; a pattern needing a new primitive must first argue that primitive into the owning chapter (T-5/T-12), then compose.
- CONFIRMATION: Each part's own law; this card adds no event.
- INVALIDATION: A pattern card restating break, retest, objective, or grading machinery fails one-rule-one-home; a candidate treated as evidence anywhere fails review.
- WHY IT WORKS: The field's pattern chapters fail by fission — every formation grows private triggers, private targets, private folklore, until the book is thirty small books that disagree. Composition is the cure: one chain, one grammar, one ledger, many names. The names earn their keep only as *statistical categories* — rows in tables — which is all they ever mechanically were.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**P-2 — THE PATTERN-IN-STRUCTURE GATE (CONTEXT IS PART OF THE DEFINITION)**

- RULE: Every pattern card declares its **structural precondition** as part of the definition sequence — read from the ledgers at candidacy, not judged: reversal compositions (P-4, P-5) require a live or just-died opposing S-4 verdict within `pat.contextLookback` bars (a "top" with no prior up-trend is congestion wearing a costume); continuation compositions (P-6) require the impulse leg their projection cites (S-8's machinery); the rectangle inherits the range's own birth law (no extra gate). The precondition filters **candidacy** — bookkeeping, not activity, so no A-6/X-4 burden arises — and the trigger chain itself obeys X-4's lookup like any chain: patterns get no activity exemption in any regime, ever. Where a reversal pattern's reference sits at the structure floor/ceiling (the usual case — an H&S neckline is ordinarily the last HL's neighborhood), its chain is the void path and is always live (X-4's falsifiability clause).
- CONFIRMATION: The precondition's objects at candidacy t_close, from stamped ledgers.
- INVALIDATION: A candidate stamped without its precondition is void ab initio; a pattern chain fired around the lookup is malformed.
- WHY IT WORKS: Bulkowski's own tables say the same shape performs differently by what preceded it — a double top after a trend and a double top inside chop are different populations. Folding context into the definition makes the name mean one population, which is what makes its table honest.
- STATUS: DRAFT

## BLOCK TWO — THE COMPOSITIONS

---

**P-3 — THE RECTANGLE (THE ALIAS, HONORED)**

- RULE: The **rectangle** *is* a MATURE range (R-5) under Chapter 7's machinery — R-9's alias, cashed: same birth, boundaries, NMZ, defense classes, death, and failed-break machinery, with nothing duplicated. This card adds only the pattern-ledger row: trigger = the range-boundary chain (already B-1's), objective = OBJ-H range height (already B-6's), volume signature = the boundary-episode columns (Q-5/Q-7), busted = R-7's LOADED-OPPOSITE feeding A-4 (already carded). Any statement about rectangles that is not a citation to Chapter 7 is an amendment to Chapter 7 and must be argued there.
- CONFIRMATION: R-5's maturity at a live range.
- INVALIDATION: A "rectangle" card diverging from range law fails review on its face.
- WHY IT MATTERS: This is the alias that proves the architecture: the book's most-traded "pattern" costs zero new cards. A pattern that is a range gets the range's rules, not a costume — the promise made at R-9, kept here.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**P-4 — DOUBLE & TRIPLE TOPS AND BOTTOMS (THE M/W FAMILY)**

- RULE: **Definition sequence (double top):** an **EQ-H pair** (S-2: consecutive same-side entries within `struct.eqTol` — the equality grammar inherited unchanged, per the R3-R-4 routing this card now cashes) after the P-2 precondition (live/just-died TRENDING(up)), with the intervening confirmed swing low's level (L-1 object) as the **neckline reference**. Triples: an EQ-H run of three (`pat.mwCount` = 3 variant); bottoms: mirror on EQ-L. **Candidate-invalidation:** any close beyond the extremes' zone before the trigger (a fresh HH — the trend resumed; the candidate never existed). **Trigger:** the B-1 chain through the neckline level's zone (reference type HORIZONTAL LEVEL — everything downstream is standard). **Grades at candidacy and claim (witnesses, never gates):** DIV-HIGH at the second extreme (I-9), reject anatomy at the extremes (E-4), the second extreme's episode volume versus the first (Q-5's grammar — the classical lighter-second-top as a column), E-2 close quality on the claim bar. **Objective:** OBJ-H formation height (extreme→neckline, §L). Busted per P-8.
- CONFIRMATION: The composition's constituent confirmations (all t_close facts); the trigger per C-2.
- INVALIDATION: The candidate-invalidation close, `pat.candidateClock` expiry, or — post-trigger — the chain's own C-3/C-6/A-2 machinery, untouched.
- WHY IT WORKS: The M/W family is the crowd failing twice at one price — an EQ-run is the mechanical residue of that failure, and the neckline is where the failed side's inventory finally concedes. The book had every part of this on the shelf; the name just points at the assembly.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**P-5 — HEAD & SHOULDERS (TOP AND INVERSE)**

- RULE: **Definition sequence (top):** under P-2's precondition, the confirmed swing sequence prints HH (head over left shoulder) then **LH** (right shoulder — the sequence's first failure, S-2's event doing the detecting); the **neckline** = a D-1 diagonal through the two intervening confirmed lows, with `diag.roleSlope` = **BOTH permitted** — the pattern-local override argued here as D's deposit required: a neckline's role comes from the formation's geometry, not from the slope-direction convention that governs standalone trendlines; it remains a full D-object otherwise (immutable anchors D-6, slope limits D-3, zone per L-4, behavior via D-4). Inverse: mirror. **Candidate-invalidation:** a close beyond the head's zone pre-trigger. **Trigger:** the B-1 chain against the neckline (reference type DIAGONAL). Note for honesty: the right-shoulder low is ordinarily the trend's last HL, so the neckline chain is typically the BOS chain wearing a name — the pattern adds the head/shoulder *categorization* (a population for the tables), not a new event; where neckline and floor diverge, both ledgers stamp per B-9's multi-reference law. **Grades:** shoulder-volume decline (left > head > right as Q-5 columns), E-2 quality at the neckline claim, DIV-HIGH across head/right-shoulder. **Objective:** OBJ-H head-to-neckline (§L).
- CONFIRMATION: Constituent confirmations; C-2 at the neckline.
- INVALIDATION: As stated; and an H&S "called" from unconfirmed swings or a hand-fit neckline (D-9) is void.
- WHY IT WORKS: H&S is the sequence-failure pattern — the first LH after an HH is structure's earliest countable crack, and the neckline is that crack given a price. Naming it buys a population with sixty years of measured statistics (the seed tables), which is the only thing the name is for.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**P-6 — FLAGS, PENNANTS & TRIANGLES (THE CONTINUATION CONTAINERS)**

- RULE: All three are **containers after an impulse**, differing only in wall geometry; one card, shared machinery, per-variant parameters. **Precondition:** an impulse leg (S-8's leg machinery) of ≥ `flag.poleMin` (VOLU units) ending within `flag.gapBars` of container start. **FLAG** = a validated counter-slope channel (D-5; slope against the pole per D-1's roles, duration ≤ `flag.maxBars`). **TRIANGLE** = two validated converging boundaries: symmetric (two D-objects), ascending (flat top: a LEVEL boundary + rising D-support), descending (mirror) — boundary defense classes port per R-4's grammar (episode classification is L-12 machinery, legal on any reference — the CH 7 deposit argued and cashed). **PENNANT** = a short triangle — the same card with `tri.pennantMaxBars`; it shares everything and *says so* (no fourth machinery). **Apex law (triangles):** the candidate expires untriggered at `apex.frac` of the distance to apex — the classical doctrine as a parameter, and expiry is candidate death, not an event. **Trigger:** the container-boundary chain in either direction (CHANNEL BOUNDARY / DIAGONAL / LEVEL type per the wall) — with-pole breaks are the continuation population, against-pole breaks route to P-8's busted machinery with premium trap context (against-WALK where D-7 stamped it). **Objectives:** flags/pennants OBJ-SW pole projection; triangles OBJ-H widest height (§L). **Volume signature:** contraction through the container, expansion at exit (Q-2/Q-7 columns — the classical claim as a measured column, never a requirement).
- CONFIRMATION: Constituent validations (D-2's defended episodes per wall); C-2 at exit.
- INVALIDATION: Container walls broken pre-validation → no candidate existed; apex/duration expiry; post-trigger, the chain's own law.
- WHY IT WORKS: A continuation container is the market renting a smaller auction inside a bigger conviction — the pole says who is winning, the container says the winners are resting, not leaving (X-2 names the state TREND-IN-RANGE when both ledgers are live, and the stamp rides the chain). Three names, one mechanism, one table family.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**P-7 — WEDGES (RISING AND FALLING)**

- RULE: **Definition:** two validated converging D-objects **sloping the same direction** (both rising = rising wedge; both falling = mirror) — the geometry that distinguishes wedges from P-6's containers; no pole precondition (wedges print as both continuations and terminal structures — the *classical reversal read is a claim for the tables, not a definition ingredient*). Candidate-invalidation: apex law as P-6. **Trigger:** the boundary chain, either direction; direction × prior-context populate the ledger rows (wedge-with-trend vs wedge-against). **Objective:** OBJ-H widest height default; OBJ-SW admissible in the sweep. **Grades:** DRYING-UP across the converging tests (Q-5 — the wedge's classical volume story as a column), divergence stamps at the terminal touches.
- CONFIRMATION: Constituent validations; C-2 at the wall.
- INVALIDATION: As P-6's container law.
- WHY IT WORKS: The wedge is convergence *with a lean* — both sides conceding ground in the same direction, which the folklore reads as exhaustion. The book declines to assume the folklore and instead gives the lean its own table rows; if the reversal read is real, the busted-vs-triggered columns will say so without the definition ever having promised it.
- STATUS: DRAFT

## BLOCK THREE — FAILURE AND THE LEDGER

---

**P-8 — THE BUSTED-PATTERN LAW (FIRST-CLASS, NOT FOOTNOTE)**

- RULE: A pattern's failure is the standard machinery with the pattern's stamp attached — nothing new, everything named: a **voided trigger** (C-3) is an A-3 fakeout setup carrying pattern context (PAT-BUSTED(name) joins LOADED-OPPOSITE/against-WALK as trigger context); a **confirmed-then-failed** trigger runs A-2's verdict reset; and the **busted continuation** — the against-pole container exit (P-6/P-7) — is the trap population the aftermath chapter was built for. The empirically loaded fact rides as a *measured claim, never a lean*: the external seed tables report busted patterns frequently outperforming their parents (Bulkowski's busted statistics — the trapped crowd is the fuel, A-3's thesis in pattern costume); `pat.bustedOdds` publishes it per pattern × regime, loop-filled, UNMEASURED until then.
- CONFIRMATION: The constituent C-3/A-2/A-3 events, unchanged.
- INVALIDATION: Any busted-specific machinery invented here fails one-rule-one-home.
- WHY IT WORKS: The busted pattern is the fakeout with a famous name — the more public the composition, the more crowded its trigger, the richer its failure. The book gives the failure equal standing with the pattern because that is where its own bias says the money is (B-8's routing bias, kept).
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**P-9 — THE PATTERN LEDGER (SEEDS DECLARED, PROMOTION EARNED)**

- RULE: Per pattern (and per variant): trigger rate from candidacy · objective hit rate (B-6 columns, §L bases) · throwback rate (A-1's machinery) · busted rate and busted-follow-through (P-8) · volume-signature lifts (the classical claims priced) · all × the regime column pair (X-9) and class tag. **The external-seed law:** Bulkowski's encyclopedia values may seed otherwise-empty cells, stamped **SEED-EXT** with vintage and source — a seeded cell may be *stated, never recommended* (B-11's law extended: external numbers are testimony from another instrument set and era, not this book's measurement); only loop-filled cells on the calibration class promote a claim past stated. A pattern whose loop columns stay flat against its seed columns is a candidate for the exclusion list by evidence (T-11 in reverse).
- CONFIRMATION: Every published pattern rate resolves to a cell with vintage, source class (SEED-EXT vs LOOP), state and class tags.
- INVALIDATION: A seeded cell consumed as a recommendation, or a naked pattern claim, fails review.
- WHY IT WORKS: Pattern lore is the field's largest inventory of unaudited numbers — this chapter inherits the best external audit that exists, labels it as testimony, and makes the machine earn the promotion. The name's whole value is its row; the row's whole value is its vintage.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**P-10 — THE EXCLUSION LIST (WHAT THIS CHAPTER REFUSES)**

- RULE: Named and excluded from v1, each with its ground, re-admission per T-11 (stated rule + evidence): **cup-and-handle, rounding tops/bottoms** (no compileable definition — "roundness" has no confirmed-swing sequence; a kernel-regression restatement would need V-14-safe machinery argued into Chapter 1 first) · **broadening formations** (diverging boundaries flunk D-2 validation economics — each touch is farther from the last; admissible only with evidence the class predicts) · **harmonic/Fibonacci-ratio patterns (Gartley family)** (ratio predicates are testable in principle, but the compositions multiply free parameters faster than any table can pay for — T-11's bar plus Aronson's discipline; the S-8 RD-FIB bands remain the book's only Fibonacci citizens) · **candlestick formations beyond Chapter 2's anatomy** (E-1..E-7 already own bar anatomy; a named two-candle "pattern" is an E-composition and may be proposed as one, not as a new family). The list is append-only in both directions: additions with grounds, removals with evidence.
- CONFIRMATION: Editorial at review; the list is checked against incoming proposals.
- INVALIDATION: An excluded name appearing in analysis under this book's name fails review.
- WHY IT MATTERS: A pattern chapter is judged by what it declines — every admission is a permanent tax on the testing surface and the reader's attention. Ten compositions with honest tables beat forty names with folklore, which is the three-iron-rules doctrine applied to the chapter most tempted to break it.
- STATUS: DRAFT
