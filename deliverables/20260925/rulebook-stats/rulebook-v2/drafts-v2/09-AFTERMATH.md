## BLOCK ONE — THE RETEST

---

**A-1 — RETEST MACHINERY (THE CLOCKS PRICED)**

- RULE: After a C-2 confirmed break of reference P (any of B-1's five types): the first TAG of P's zone within `retest.clock` bars opens **RETEST**; it resolves **HOLD** (a CLOSE BEYOND away from the zone, in the break's direction, within `retest.confirmClock`) or **FAIL** (a CLOSE BEYOND back through the zone against the break) — V-24's exact events, now with their test ranges: `retest.clock` 3–15, `retest.confirmClock` 2–5, `fake.clock` 2–5 (the deposit paid). No tag within the clock → **NO-RETEST** (feeds B-7's runaway test). A HOLD fires ENTRY-R (B-5) and completes the flip machinery (L-9). Retest base rates (HOLD/FAIL frequency by reference type × grade) live in the aftermath ledger (A-10).
- CONFIRMATION: Each resolution's defining close at t_close.
- INVALIDATION: A retest read from a tag outside the clock, or a HOLD read from a close that never left the zone, is void.
- WHY IT WORKS: The retest is the market auditing its own break — the broken side gets one chance to prove the new price wrong at the old price. Bulkowski's tables say the audit happens more often than not; this card is where the book stops narrating it and starts clocking it.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**A-2 — RETEST-FAIL: THE VERDICT RESET**

- RULE: FAIL fires the break verdict's **void_event** — the C-2 verdict dies on the record, stamped; the reference reverts BROKEN → ACTIVE (L-9); and the resets are exactly these: the break verdict and every grade annotation riding it. **Nothing else resets:** a level's retest-fail does not touch a live trend verdict (S-4) — unless the reference *was* the structure floor/ceiling, in which case S-7's machinery already governed the larger death. The fail additionally stamps **TRAPPED(break-direction)**: everyone who entered on ENTRY-C, and any early ENTRY-R, is inventoried on the wrong side of a re-defended price — the context A-3 spends. **[R5-R-1]** This void is one of the break verdict's two retirement paths — the finite `cfm.verdictClock` stands alongside it, whichever fires first (ratified; the law lives in C-2 v3).
- CONFIRMATION: The FAIL close at t_close.
- INVALIDATION: A verdict reset without its FAIL event, or a reset cascading to verdicts not on this card's list, corrupts the record (V-21's ledger discipline).
- WHY IT WORKS: Exactly which beliefs die when a break fails is the question the field answers with a shrug. The verdict object makes it a list, and the list is short on purpose — most of the chart didn't change; the trapped crowd did.
- STATUS: DRAFT

## BLOCK TWO — THE TRAPS

---

**A-3 — THE FAKEOUT SETUP (C-3'S VOID, SPENT)**

- RULE: The book's first full **setup card** — a tradeable event sequence with all five parts: **TRIGGER** = a C-3 void at any of the five reference types (the claim died unconfirmed), carrying its context stamps (LOADED-OPPOSITE at range boundaries, against-WALK at channel boundaries, failed-structural-attack at floors/ceilings, TRAPPED from A-2 when arriving via retest-fail). **DIRECTION** = opposite the failed claim. **ENTRY** per `fake.entryMode`: IMMEDIATE (the void close itself) or CONF (the next close continuing against the failed claim). **INVALIDATION** = a close beyond the failed claim's excursion extreme (the attacker's furthest point — if the market goes back through it, the trap thesis is dead), within the setup's life. **OBJECTIVE** = B-6's grammar, default OBJ-NEXT (for range boundaries: the opposite boundary — OBJ-H equivalent per R-7). **CLOCK** = `fake.setupClock` bars; unresolved, the setup expires. Honesty: `fake.setupOdds` by reference type × defense class × grade (A-10).
- CONFIRMATION: The trigger void at t_close; entry per the declared mode.
- INVALIDATION: The stated invalidation close — mirror-precise per C-4 — or clock expiry.
- WHY IT WORKS: The void is the chain's cruelest information: a crowd committed beyond a defended price and was closed back through it. Their exits are fuel in the opposite direction, and unlike most "signals," the trap's mechanism — forced unwinding — has an identifiable payer. Brooks built a career on this event; here it is with its clocks and its table.
- STATUS: DRAFT

---

**A-4 — SPRING & UPTHRUST (THE NAMED PAIR, CARDED)**

- RULE: **SPRING** = A-3 instantiated at a live range's **lower** boundary: trigger = the C-3 void below the range low (R-7's LOADED-OPPOSITE stamp is the context), direction = up, entry per `fake.entryMode`, **invalidation = a close below the void excursion's low** (the spring's low is the line — one close under it and the spring is dead), objective = the opposite boundary (OBJ-H: the range's height, the classical target). **UPTHRUST** = the exact mirror at the upper boundary. Premium context, ledgered: range maturity (R-5) and a VOID-DEFENSE-rich boundary record (R-4) grade the setup up; **volume upgrades JOINED (CH 10 cashed):** CLIMAX(side) on the void bar is the setup's top volume grade, with DRYING-UP(n) on the boundary's prior episodes as the textbook pre-context (Q-6/Q-5 via Q-8 — Wyckoff's spring *is* a selling climax that failed to break the floor). These are A-3 with names because the range gives the trap a *known* objective and a *known* payer — the tightest version of the trade this book knows.
- CONFIRMATION: The R-7 event at the boundary; entries per A-3.
- INVALIDATION: As stated — and a spring read at a dead or dissolved range (R-6) is malformed; the container must be live.
- WHY IT WORKS: Wyckoff's pair, Pruden's formalization, this book's clocks: the failed break *of a mature range* traps the crowd against the full width of a proven auction. The opposite boundary isn't a hope — it is where the other half of the auction has been paying for weeks.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**A-5 — THE GAP TRAP (E-14'S ANNOTATION, SPENT)**

- RULE: Trigger = a **FAILED-BREAKAWAY** finalization (E-14: a breakaway-class gap filled within `gap.exhClock`). Direction = against the gap. Entry = the fill-confirming close (the E-9 FILLED event's bar), or CONF mode per `fake.entryMode`. **Invalidation** = a close beyond the gap bar's far extreme (the O_t side — the trap thesis dies if the market re-crosses the entire gap). Objective = B-6's grammar, default OBJ-NEXT. Context stamps: the crossed level's record (the gap jumped it without a test, C-8 — the market has now come back for the skipped inventory). **Volume upgrade (CH 10 cashed):** CLIMAX on the fill-confirming bar grades the trap up (Q-6 via Q-8 — the crowd's capitulation is the entry's fuel). Honesty: gap-trap odds by gap size and crossed-level quality (A-10).
- CONFIRMATION: The E-14 finalization at t_close.
- INVALIDATION: As stated; a gap trap read on a COMMON or CONTINUATION finalization is malformed — only the failed breakaway traps a committed crowd.
- WHY IT WORKS: The overnight crowd paid a premium to reprice past a defended level without a fight — and the fill means the fight found them anyway, at worse prices, with the whole gap as their drawdown. The most leveraged trap in the book because the entry (the gap open) was the most confident.
- STATUS: DRAFT

**A-6 — WHIPSAW & THE STAND-DOWN**

- RULE: `after.whipsawCount` terminal voids (C-3) in **alternating directions** at the same reference within `after.whipsawWindow` bars → the reference enters **WHIPSAW** state: claim-opening against it is **suspended** for `after.standDownClock` bars. This is the book's rare activity gate, and it is legal because it gates rule *activity*, never verdict truth (the invariance boundary holds); its ancestor is the unclear-regime default (X-1's principle — fewer rules when the tape is incoherent), and its formal regime home arrives with CH 13 (deposit). Existing verdicts and clocks run out undisturbed. **[R5-R-2] The exception is unique and self-enforcing:** this card is the book's **sole sanctioned activity gate** — any future card wanting to suspend rule activity must argue explicitly against this card's standard (activity, never truth; mechanical incoherence evidence only; claims in flight always resolve) and clear the same bar. CH 13's regime machinery, when it formalizes the stand-down's home, carries that burden by name. *(Carried — X-4 argues it on the record, clause by clause; A-6 remains the sole reference-local gate, X-4 the sole regime-scope gate, and any third gate now argues against both.)*
- CONFIRMATION: The alternating-void count at t_close.
- INVALIDATION: A stand-down suppressing an already-open claim's resolution (claims in flight always resolve), or extended by anything but the clock, is malformed.
- WHY IT WORKS: Two opposite crowds trapped at one price in quick succession means the reference isn't refereeing anything — it is noise wearing a level's ledger. The cheapest response to incoherence is scheduled silence.

**A-7 — THE POST-FLIP THIRD TEST (SINGLE HOME, COMPLETED)**

- RULE: After a FLIP (L-9), the flipped level's episode ledger counts fresh (the flip is a rebirth of role — L-5 episodes accrue from the HOLD). The classical claim — repeated tests of a *flipped* level fail more often than tests of a native one — publishes as the honesty table `after.flipTestOdds[n]` (hold rate by post-flip episode count, by original break grade), loop-filled. L-7 owns pre-break test odds; this card owns post-flip; the split the assessment ordered (§5) is now complete on both sides, cross-referenced, never restated.
- CONFIRMATION: Episode counting from the HOLD event.
- INVALIDATION: Post-flip counts contaminated by pre-break episodes (the ledger distinguishes lives) are corrupt.
- WHY IT WORKS: A flipped level is defended by converts — the trapped crowd that switched sides — and converts have shallower pockets than natives. Whether that folk wisdom is true is exactly what the table will say.

**A-8 — WITHDRAWAL & CONTEST EVIDENCE (E'S DEPOSITS, JOINED)**

- RULE: The aftermath's grade joins (B-4's pattern, aftermath side): a retest HOLD accompanied by an E-4 reject at the zone grades up (REJECT-DEFENDED class); a **reject-withdrawal** (the next close re-enters the zone, E-4's stated withdrawal) or an **engulf-withdrawal** (E-6's giveback) at the retest grades it down; an **outside bar** (E-5) on the retest bar stamps CONTESTED — two-sided stop-outs in one session, downgrading confidence in *both* directions' next moves. All grades, never gates (C-9/C-10 discipline throughout).
- CONFIRMATION: The constituent E events at their t_closes.
- INVALIDATION: Withdrawal evidence read across an intervening episode boundary (stale anatomy) is void.
- WHY IT WORKS: The bar-level tells at a retest are the auction's fine print — who showed up, who was refused, who got run. E defined them; this card seats them at the one table where they change the odds that matter.

**A-9 — POST-OBJECTIVE LAW (PAID, AND THE ANTI-GREED RULE)**

- RULE: When an objective tags (B-6's PAID event): the break's projection is settled — the stamp is permanent, and **no extension may be projected from the same break**: further expectation requires a fresh reference and a fresh chain (new claim, new confirmation — the anti-greed law). Post-PAID behavior generates ordinary events on the standing references (the objective price itself, if it coincides with a reference, runs that reference's ledger; an objective that is merely a projected price births nothing — projections are not levels). CH 16 consumes PAID as its scale-out knob site (deposit honored).
- CONFIRMATION: The PAID tag at t_close.
- INVALIDATION: An "extended target" published from a paid break without a new chain fails review.
- WHY IT WORKS: The measured move is a claim about trapped inventory's unwind distance — once paid, the payer is gone. Every further dollar is a new thesis, and the book makes it buy its own evidence.
