How it was measured

  • TRAILING P/E is the price over the last twelve months of REPORTED earnings (the last four quarters of diluted GAAP EPS, each quarter counted 45 days after it ends, from fundamentals_history). FORWARD P/E is the price over the NEXT twelve months of CONSENSUS earnings (the next four quarterly analyst estimates after the last report, FMP consensus); estimate history is not stored, so forward is today only and has no chart. Each fund's P/E is built from its own holdings: today's holdings (FMP, 26-28 Sep 2026) held as fixed share counts, the fund's P/E being the total price of those shares over their total earnings - the index way. Coverage is the share of the fund's weight with both prices and quarterly EPS (names whose EPS is in another currency than their price are left out). Survivorship: today's holdings are carried back in time, so names that left or failed are missing and today's winners are in from the start. Months where the fund's total earnings were near zero (QQQ and SMH in 2003, SPY in 2009) give off-scale P/Es; they are real arithmetic, not a reading. Today's point uses October's closes moved by each fund's own move to the 20 Nov close.
