02 · Where we stand

How far each one is below its record

Each bar is the fall from its highest close. The line under the bar is the distance from its 200-day average, the long-term trend line. For US stocks, now is today's live price.

03 · Next tests

The reports and numbers that decide the next move

In date order. An outlined row is the report's estimate; a filled row is a confirmed date. Where we follow the company, the report's date is checked against Scintilla's own earnings calendar.

04 · Company by company

When the bad news lands, and when it turns

Each mark sits on the day the news becomes public. Red is bad news, amber is the margin low, purple is a peak, green is a turn up. A hollow mark is the tougher path, where it differs. The strip on top counts the marks each month: red bad, green good.

05 · Gross margin

Gross margin is the share of each sales dollar left after the cost of making the product. The tick is the company's high since 2019, the dot is the latest report and the ring is its next guide. A red line means the latest is below the high.

06 · The big buyers

The five big buyers keep growing on analysts' numbers

Sales growth against a year earlier, each quarter: solid bars are reported, outlined bars are analysts' average. Under each: 2027 sales growth on three paths, and how much of the added sales came from the cloud business.

07 · GPU renters

Each bar is the report's odds that the company restructures its debt by the end of 2028. The ring is the same odds on the Fed-hike path. The four lights say whether its stated plans are funded on each path.

08 · The report against what we track

Every company in the report, beside what Scintilla tracks

One row per company. The left half is what Scintilla tracks: the Geiger (−1 to +1), how far the live price is below its 1-year high, analysts' average target against today's price, and the last four quarters of sales growth. The right half is what the report says: the margin, its next dated mark, the turn, and the next report date checked against our calendar. Click a row for the full detail.
