What the season says so far

  1. Beats are a given; guidance moves stocks. 17 of 19 beat on EPS, yet NFLX, ISRG, GM, GE, DHR and TMO are all lower. Nothing is being paid for a beat with a softer outlook.
  2. Input inflation is the theme. Brent around $111, steel and copper +50%, and memory chips have all reached the outlook lines: airlines cut guidance, GM's 2027 is conditional, GE's margin is flat. Pricing power (MMM, UAL fares) is the defense.
  3. The consumer is holding. Card credit is benign (COF, SYF), fares are up 16% and still filling planes, and holiday spending is on pace. The improvement is flattening, though, not accelerating.
  4. Rates are a fresh headwind for banks. The Fed is hiking, the 10-year rose 82bp in the quarter, deposit costs are rising faster, and bond losses are pausing buybacks (HWC).
  5. Clean winners: defense, the aero aftermarket and anything touching the AI build (3M connectors, RTX parts scarcity, memory pricing). AI is now crowding out other industries' component supply.
